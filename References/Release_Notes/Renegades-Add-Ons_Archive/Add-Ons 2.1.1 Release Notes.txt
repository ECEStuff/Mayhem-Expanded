The mod has been updated to 2.1.1. 

Previous savegames compatibility: PARTIAL*. 
Previous galaxies compatibility: YES.

*Notes:
- New planetary effects are available only in new games.

Highlights
- Expanded Terraforming with new planetary effects and the new Anomalous rarity.
  - Anomalous planets require Terraforming efficiency 3 to terraform.
  - Added Barren, Molten, Toxic, Gaia, and Tomb Worlds.
- Added new Population-to-Research and Research-to-Population terraforming effects.
- Coordinated invasions can now prioritize sectors with terraformed Rare or Anomalous planets, as well as sectors with 2+ terraformed planets (previously 3+).
- Recycling time is now displayed and can be reduced by Toxic Worlds.
- Fixed several Outpost population, production, and ship construction inconsistencies.
- Improved Terraforming, Logistics, and Production Capacity information.

-----------------------------------------
		ADDITIONS
-----------------------------------------
- New terraforming rarity: Anomalous
  - This new rarity reinforces that terraforming rarity represents how unusual and specialized an effect is rather than raw superiority.	
  - This rarity adds some terraforming challenges, further encouraging long-term planning.
  - These planets require Terraforming efficiency 3. 
  - New anomalous terraforming effects:
    - Their effects provide specialized benefits rather than simply being stronger versions of lower-rarity effects.
    - Barren World: generic terraforming bonus is +35% instead of +25%.
    - Toxic World: +50% recycling speed.
    - Molten World: +30 Ore and +30 Silicon per minute. Materials are given once every Outpost consumption cycle.

- New Uncommon and Rare terraforming effects:
  - The 2 new uncommon effects were added to diversify the uncommon pool, ensuring there are more uncommons than rares but fewer uncommons than commons.
  - Population-to-Research: uncommon effect
    - Gain +1% research rate for every 5 maximum population.
    - This adds a scaling effect that rewards high-population sectors.
  - Research-to-Population: uncommon effect
    - Gain +1 maximum population for every 5% research rate. Excludes Faster Research. 
    - This adds a scaling effect that rewards high-research sectors.
  - Gaia World: rare effect
    - Can spawn only in de jure and Terran Memory sectors.
    - Generic terraforming bonus is +50% instead of +25%.
  - Tomb World: rare effect
    - Periodically salvages all four shipbuilding materials, but provides no generic terraforming bonus.
    - Every 30 minutes, salvages 100% of 30 minutes of normal factory production for each shipbuilding material.
      - This translates to 600 Hull Plating, 600 Quantum Tubes, 180 Computer Components, and 180 Crystals per Tomb World.
    - Still provides the normal Perk from terraforming.
    - This provides a rare alternative to conventional shipbuilding production at the cost of sector stat growth.

		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Coordinated Invasions (CIs) can now prioritize sectors containing terraformed planets with rare or anomalous terraforming effects.
  - CIs can also select sectors with 2+ terraformed planets (previously 3+ terraformed planets). This reduces overlap with the existing high-station-count targeting condition, allowing heavily terraformed sectors to become attractive invasion targets earlier.
  - Rare and Anomalous terraforming effects make these sectors strategically valuable and should factor into invasion targeting.

- Recycling time is now displayed. 
  - Previously, only a vague "will take some time" message prompted the player.
  - This allows players to plan around recycling times and clearly see the benefit of Toxic Worlds.
