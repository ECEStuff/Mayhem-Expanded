Adds epic-tier terraforming perks, revamps the existing rarity system for terraforming effects, adds terraforming milestone perks to outposts, and formalizes Renegades plot into the mission journal.

Playable Kha'ak ships
- Since M6 and up are already available by bailing, their fighters are now bailable.
- Kha'ak M3/M4/M5 can bail but 20% as often as normal M3/M4/M5.
- These ships are recyclable, but no research progress will be given. As such, these ships are not buildable.

Terraforming revamp phase 3
- Added color coding to common/rare/epic terraforming effects shown in Ministry of War. Changed existing category (common/epic) to common/rare/epic.
  - Common = gray (same as before), rare = cyan (formerly the epic tier introduced in phase 1), epic = magenta (new)
- Terraforming milestones
  - 1.1 introduced these benefits for OCV playthroughs (to demonstrate how the effects work). 1.2 puts them behind milestone perks, regardless of playthrough.
  - Added Terraforming efficiency 1-4 perks (TE 1-4). These are unlocked as you terraform more planets. To unlock the next tier after tier 1, you must terraform more planets and have the previous tier unlocked.
    - Tier 1: 1.2x manpower, 2.0x if you sided with the Xenon/OCV.
    - Tier 2: Global penalty reduced by 20%.
    - Tier 3: +1 concurrent project allowed in the outpost's sector.
    - Tier 4: All terraforming perks and capital shipyard required. Outpost ganis +10% base max specialization.
  - Added number of terraformed planets to top of outpost GUI.
  - Since 1.1, every 4 planets you have in your empire lets your empire support 1 additional project. As of 1.2, once you've terraformed 25 planets, you will receive a message. From then on, your empire can support 1 additional project every 3 planets instead.
- Added 6 new effects to the epic pool:
  - Academy: +5 stat points to all marine stats, stacks per academy.
  - Recycler: For each recycler, +5% more recycling factor (stacks with Capital shipyard) and +10% more research from recycling
  - +25 rastar oil per minute, given once every Outpost consumption cycle.
  - +40 teladianium per minute, given once every Outpost consumption cycle.
  - +60 ore per minute, given once every Outpost consumption cycle.
  - +60 silicon per minute, given once every Outpost consumption cycle.
