-----------------------------------------
- Satellites
  - The starting satellite now spawns in the player's cargobay rather than in the starting sector, regardless of start.
    - Previously, on non-Xenon starts, the starting satellite spawned somewhere in the starting sector.
    - Additional starting satellites (from choosing sector reveal in starting options) now spawn only where the player meets the satellite-maintenance reputation requirement.
      - Notably, this applies to Quickstart which starts the player off at 4k reputation with their origin faction.
  - On easy/normal/hard, the player now receives a tutorial message the first time a satellite is destroyed by satellite removal.
  - Rationale: People have asked why their satellites were being removed. Because periodic satellite removal is not a vanilla mechanic, it deserves its own tutorial.

  - Now repairs claimed ships to 65% hull, making them combat-ready.

- Abandoned ships
  - Fixed bailed big ships (M6, TM, M8, capital ships) spawning with slightly below the intended 5% hull.
  - Fixed black market and abandoned ships spawning slightly below the minimum 40% hull.
  - Fixed a rare race condition where tugs could claim reserved abandoned ships.
    - This was occurring by spawning abandoned ships without immediately setting the no-capture flag (mayhem_notCaptureableByTugs) upon spawning and by checking the no-capture flag only before sending the tug.
    - This flag is now set immediately after spawning (and bailing) the abandoned ship. Additionally, tugs check the no-capture flag twice: one before being sent to claim ships and once immediately before claiming.
