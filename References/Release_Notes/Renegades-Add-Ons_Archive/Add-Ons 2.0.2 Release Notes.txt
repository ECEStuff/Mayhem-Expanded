The mod has been updated to 2.0.2. Due to increasing IRL responsibilities, development is on hiatus for the next 2 months with the exception of minor bug fixes.

Highlights:
- Improved extra Xenon defenders.
- Reworked Renegade roamers and raiders.
- Reduced ascension sector requirement on smaller galaxies.
- Removed galaxy map reveal to preserve exploration.
- Fixed coalition interactions.
- Fixed missing voice line and IDs for Capital Shipyard, Terran Outpost, and Phanon Outpost.

Previous savegames compatibility: PARTIAL*. 
Previous galaxies compatibility: PARTIAL**.

Notes:
- *Existing coalitions may have incorrect diplomatic state. Starting with 2.0.2, the player's reputation is saved before joining a coalition.
- **Capital Shipyard and Phanon Outpost voice-line files require a newly generated galaxy. 
  - Existing galaxies will continue using their current 00044.xml voice references.

- Mobile factories and Instant Crafting
  - Single order reworked: no longer renders your TL stationary. 
    - Like loop production, now works behind the scenes, allowing the TL to perform other orders.
  - Instant crafting nerfed: resource costs are doubled compared to mobile factories.
  - Kha'ak ships, Phanon M3s, and Phanon M4s are now considered mobile factory candidates.
  - Jump Beacon variable (9972 t-file, variable 174) is now a toggle. 
    - If enabled and on easy mode, reduces the Jumpdrive Core cost of Jump Beacons by 2. 
    - Only for new games. Does not apply to normal, hard, and insane difficulties.
  - Mobile Factory limit (9972 t-file, variable 273) is now a toggle.
    - Now an optional modifier on easy but required on normal and up. 
    - Limits the number of Mobile Factories; the limit is proportional to how many planets are in the player's empire.
  - Outpost GUI now displays the available resource quantities for crafting hackerchips and jump beacons (special items).
  - Outpost GUIs now use Mobile Factory cost definitions as the source of truth, preventing cost mismatches.
  - Rationale: 
    - In all other cases, loop production was superior to the old mobile factory due to preserving resources.
    - Previously, instant crafting completely bypassed the Mobile Factory's time/resource balance, making Mobile Factory obsolete.
    - Instant crafting cost is now doubled to ensure that the Mobile Factory remains the primary production source for special items, while keeping instant crafting available for true emergencies.

- Renegades
  - Reworked roamer and raider behavior.
    - Can now spawn in ANY non-player sector except the Renegade sector. Previously, Yaki and Xenon (non-OCV alignment) sectors were ineligible.
    - They now relocate to other sectors after extended inactivity. Roamers' fleeing behavior is unchanged.
    - Roamers trapped by player territory now transition into raiders.
    - Raiders now relocate between player sectors when no targets remain.
    - Target selection has been revised to reduce ineffective pursuits and worker (explorer, tug, looter) exploits.
      - Target selection is now determined solely by ship class, speed, and current distance. Renegades now choose targets they can reasonably engage.
      - For speed, it uses the attacker's current max speed + 20% or 25m/s, whichever is greater. 
      - For distance, it uses the attacker's range + 25%. 
        - In both cases, this is to give roamers and raiders some margin to engage targets that are nearby and respects OOS mechanics. This should result in baiting being less effective while still giving roamers and raiders meaningful targets to engage.

  - Renegades are now color-coded and named by role. Coloring was a scrapped feature from base Renegades.
    - Assassins and Defenders rename and recolor themselves as raiders when they turn into raiders!
    - Raider: red
    - Roamer: yellow
    - Assassin: magenta
    - Defender: blue

  - Renegade flee chance is now determined upon creation. A TM gains an additional +15% flat chance (previously +25% flat chance). Renegade roamer flee chance is now as follows and is affected by scaled difficulty:
    - 25% on easy (up from 20%)
    - 35% on normal (up from 25%)
    - 45% on hard (up from 35%)
    - 55% on insane (up from 50%)
    - 65% on insane+1 (up from 50%)
    - 75% on insane+2 (never happens, as roamers at this point will always raid the player)

  - Rationale:
    - Older Renegade roamers had a chance to flee determined upon killing satellites, and roamers stayed in the same location.
    - Players could exploit predictable roamer behavior through savescumming or by tracking their locations, giving players more opportunities than intended to intercept them.
    - For the flee chance, this allows scaled difficulty to affect roamers. The chances are higher since roamer behavior is determined upon creation rather than upon each satellite kill. The increased flee chance should compensate for the anti-savescum measure.
    - For relocation, this allows roamers and raiders to reposition, ensuring that the player cannot "bank" Renegades. This also solves edge cases where 2+ roamers can occupy the same sector.
    - For target selection, this ensures that workers cannot be used as spotters.
    - These changes overall ensure that Renegades are less predictable, preventing players from permanently neutralizing roamers and raiders through avoidance or savescumming. They also require the player to commit to interception.

- Ascension 
  - Changed sector requirement: it is now 20% (30% on insane) of the galaxy or 20 (30) sectors, whichever is lower.
  - Rationale:
    - Faction territory count scales with galaxy size, but the previous sector requirement did not.
    - On smaller galaxies, sector discovery can become disproportionately difficult due to hostile patrols and limited unexplored space.
    - The requirement now scales with galaxy size, making completion more consistent across different galaxy setups.

- Starting Options
  - Removed reveal galaxy map option from chaotic start, starting options, and quickstart.
  - Rationale:
    - Exploration is one of the core pillars of a 4X game.
    - Revealing the entire galaxy at game start granted perfect strategic information from the start and reduced the value of exploration.
    - Players must now discover the galaxy themselves, making information a resource that must be earned and aligning Quickstart with other gamestarts.

- Faction dynamics: coalitions
  - Players can no longer join another coalition if they are already in a coalition.
  - Players can no longer declare permanent war against coalition allies.
  - Destroying or boarding a coalition ally's assets results in an expulsion and a 12 hour suspension from joining other coalitions.
  - Player reputation is now saved prior to joining a coalition, meaning the player's old reputation is restored after the coalition disbands.
  - Rationale:
    - Coalitions are a political dynamic where AI relations were being restored but not the player's.
    - Prior this patch, disbanding the coalition could bring the player's reputation to a better state or an even worse state than before the coalition.
    - These changes ensure that the player's permanent reputation is unaffected and consequences for violating coalitions, since coalitions force temporary friendship until it disbands.

- Xenon/OCV
  - The maelstrom sector is now a candidate for extra Xenon defenders if it has more than 2 gates (the maelstrom counts as a dead gate).
    - If the maelstrom sector is selected, then the extra Xenon fleet spawns at the portal. The fleet is an elite composition of OCV and Xenon ships.
  - OCV spawn faster if the maelstrom sector has more than 2 gates. Cycle time is now also dependent on difficulty.
  - OCV fighters and M6 now spawn in batches. Capital ships still spawn 1 at a time.
  - Initial OCV spawns are now based on difficulty, with hard and insane spawning more enemies. 
    - Increased OCV quota by 1 M7 for harder difficulties and 1 M2 insane difficulty before OCV commits to an invasion.
  - Extra Xenon defenders have been reworked:
    - The amounts per ship type have been increased, but strongholds (Xenon sectors adjacent to the portal sector) spawn 1 less M7 and 1 less M2.
    - Stations now track their defender leader rather than being given a garrison once per lifetime.
      - If the leader dies, then its station becomes eligible to spawn a new defender fleet.
    - Xenon sectors close to the portal (2-4 jumps away) no longer have a chance to downgrade to minimal defenses.
    - An extra fleet is now guaranteed to spawn every 3 hours until the cap is reached. Previously, each cycle instead had a chance to spawn a fleet.
    - The number of garrisons is now scaled by difficulty: 
      - Easy: 0.8x
      - Normal: 1x
      - Hard: 1.5x
      - Insane: 2x (1x if player joined the OCV)
  - Rationale:
    - In existing games, Xenon stronghold garrisons could spawn excessive capital ships: 2 M2s, 2 M7s, and 1 M1 per fleet.
    - It was possible for these sectors to be wiped by roaming patrols, and it is possible for the maelstrom sector to have more than 1 active gate connection (including jump holes).
    - This allowed multiple full-strength garrisons to stack in a single sector, effectively turning some sectors into no-fly zones.
    - Conversely, once those garrisons were destroyed, their sectors could be permanently pacified.
    - These changes make Xenon sectors more consistently threatening while keeping their defenses realistically fortified. 
    - The gate criteria represents the need for extra security in the maelstrom sector when increased connectivity makes the portal sector easier for hostile fleets to reach.
    - OCV batch spawning ensures that fighters and M6 fight at full strength; these classes are frail but are strong in numbers. It also means that fighters are no longer the bottleneck for OCV ship production.
