-----------------------------------------
OCV Playthroughs
- Added Spitfyre and Ranger blueprints upon completing the main story. 
  - Rationale: This achieves parity with normal playthroughs. Also, these ships are unobtainable during OCV playthroughs (OCV can't research) and fill capability gaps.

-----------------------------------------
		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- The starting TL no longer comes with a Research Station. Instead, the Research Station comes inside the first Outpost upon placing that Outpost.
  - This reduces early-game sequencing exploits and ties the station to the intended first-colony flow.
  - For existing games where the player hasn't placed their first station (either the Outpost or Research Station), the Research Station is removed from the TL.
    - Note: if you've already placed a Research Station and then place your first Outpost, you will not get another free Research Station.

	. setup.ZH.xml
	. Mayhem.InitializeOutpost.xml
	. Mayhem.Mission.PlotTL.xml

- Supplier missions now pay significantly more if the destination is an NPC factory.
  - Rather than pay 1/3 of the shipment value at minimum price per ware (thus disincentivizing players from completing the mission by simply taking the wares), the pay is now 4/3 of the shipment value at average price per ware.
  - If the shipment is to be delivered to a player factory, then the payment is still 1/3 as much.
  - Note: does not apply to existing missions. Must be a new one.

	. Mayhem.Mission.Create.xml

- Moved shipbuilding menu tooltips to new pedia page (Empire -> Ship Rules and Traits)
  - Rationale: the shipbuilding menu was excessively long. To reduce clutter, only the most pertinent information was kept in the shipbuilding menu. The factional tooltips have been moved to their own Encyclopedia page.

  - Rationale: debug menu is meant for debugging, not to store player settings which may be frequently used and changed.
