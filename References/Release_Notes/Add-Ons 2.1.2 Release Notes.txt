Highlights
- Transferring sectors now awards +1 additional Favor Point for every 2 terraformed planets.
- Ship Purchase and Quantum Tube trading at Trading Stations are now disabled on Insane difficulty.
- Removed the resource-free factories modifier.
- Improved Favor and sector transfer information.
- Organized the 9972 and 9973 configuration files for easier navigation.
- Fixed writing and capitalization inconsistencies.

		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Transferring a sector now awards additional Favor Points if it contains terraformed planets.
  - For every 2 terraformed planets, gain 1 additional Favor Point.
  - This gives more incentive to transfer sectors with terraformed planets, as the AI can use these planets.
  - Updated tutorial to reflect this change.

- Certain features are now disabled on Insane difficulty to preserve the intended difficulty and patch loopholes:
  - Ship Purchase (9972, variable 246): this feature allows any ship class to be acquired with Loyalty, bypassing several of Insane's shipbuilding and progression restrictions.
  - Quantum Tubes in trading stations (9973, variable 102): this preserves Insane's intended shipbuilding logistics by requiring Quantum Tubes to be obtained through their normal production chain.
