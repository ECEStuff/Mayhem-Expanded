All systems optimized. The mod has been updated to 1.13.

Highlights
- Added Hacking Station Menu QoL.
- Improved OCV playthroughs.
  - Reworked economy: credits converted into red crystals.
  - Hacking updated with crystal costs instead of credits.
  - Equipment now costs colored crystals but at halved cost compared to non-OCV playthroughs.
  - 2 new perks: Combustor and Crystal Refinery.
- Automation controls added to Personal Console (autosave, auto-recycle).
- Buffed Friendly Neighbor perk: faster passive reputation recovery.

Previous savegames compatibility: YES*.
Previous galaxies compatibility: YES.

* New game is recommended. This update introduces a one-time conversion of credits into red crystals. Existing saves are supported, but starting a new game is recommended for the intended experience.

- Added 2 new perks for OCV playthrough.
  - Combustor: converts food, spaceweed, and spacefuel into energy cells.
    - A maximum of 750 energy cells can be produced from combustion. However, it requires the Solar Panels perk to be unlocked.
    - Up to 500 of each food type -> 100 energy cells per food type (total: 400 energy cells from food)
    - Up to 10 spaceweed -> 100 energy cells
    - Up to 10 spacefuel -> 250 energy cells
  - Crystal refinery: Converts colored minerals into colored crystals and crystals into red crystals.
    - Requires 100 red crystals, 10 yellow crystals, 4 blue crystals, and 1 green crystal as a one-time cost.
    - The upgrade is fully refunded when upgrading to the Capital Shipyard.
    - Converts as follows per consumption cycle:
      - Up to 60 crystals -> 12 red crystal
      - 3 red mineral -> 2 red crystal
      - 1 orange mineral -> 2 red crystal
      - 3 purple mineral -> 2 yellow crystal
      - 2 blue mineral -> 3 yellow crystal
      - 1 green mineral -> 1 blue crystal
      - 1 silver mineral -> 2 blue crystal
      - 1 gold mineral -> 1 green crystal
      - 1 white mineral -> 2 green crystal
  - Rationale:
    - Gives OCV players passive economy generation and a resource sink for now-unusable wares since they lack the ability to trade with NPCs.

		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- OCV Playthroughs
  - 75% of your total credits are now converted to red crystals. They will be stored in your fleet when it spawns at the Maelstrom. If your fleet can't store all the crystals, then the remaining crystals will spawn at the Maelstrom.
  - Boarding no longer gives credits. It instead gives red crystals.
  - Hacking is no longer free and now requires red crystals. However:
    - Accounting now drops a crate of red crystals at the hacked ship's location upon hacking that ship.
    - Virus research completes twice as fast.
    - Hacking research costs are halved.
    - Hacking global defense program costs 50% more.
  - Equipment is no longer free. Instead, equipment costs are halved. Equipment value is estimated based on red crystals.
  - To help with fleet regrouping, the free Xenon Z now comes with a Jump Beacon.
  - Unlimited salvage insurance is now enabled upon joining the OCV; the player otherwise cannot get more salvage insurance.
  - The player no longer receives credits and favor points from bounties (bounty board, priority target, hive queen, etc.)

  - Rationale:
    - OCV players don't use credits. The original idea from ZH 2.1 to make their costs free, but doing so made crystals semi-redundant (save for Capital Shipyards and OWPs) and easily allowed the player to snowball with instant hacking research.
    - Costs are halved to balance the OCV player's lack of trade while preserving the spirit behind discounts introduced in ZH 2.1.
    - The red crystal was chosen as the OCV player's base currency since it's the lowest colored crystal tier and still costs at least 2 station slots to get passive economy generation. This achieves parity with normal playthroughs by reintroducing costs.
      - Regular crystal was not chosen due to its relatively low value per credit compared to red crystals. It would take A LOT of crystals to perform hacking research if crystals were chosen as the base currency! 
    - The hacking defense program is more expensive to make up for the increased incentive to hack enemy ships.
      - Unlike boarding, which requires player intervention and micro to actively obtain new ships, hacking these ships passively fills in gaps in the player's empire.

- Reworked Pirate tug behavior:
  - Instead of simply claiming abandoned ships within 10km at 20% of the time (previously 30km at 50% of the time prior to 1.9.7), their behavior now emulates real tugs as follows:
    - Look for any captureable, abandoned ships within 30km.
    - Roll a 33% chance to attempt salvage (up from 20%).
    - Fly to within 3km before claiming.
    - Claim succeeds only if the ship remains neutral (abandoned) and the Pirate is within 6km.
    - Restore equipment and clear bail restrictions upon claiming.
  - Rationale:
    - Pirate claiming mechanic was added in Renegades 5.3. However, the initial approach was unrealistic.
      - They were allowed to remotely claim from 30km away, and that is normally not possible without Remote System Control Software.
    - This change align pirate claiming behavior with player and NPC tug mechanics, thus maintaining consistency with Mayhem's design philosophy: 
      - No remote system control without appropriate tools.
      - Parity with NPC and player behavior.

- Buffed Friendly Neighbor
  - Rather than repair 208 rep per hour, now repairs negative rep at a rate of 1500 rep per hour, provided that your reputation had not worsened since the past hour. If your reputation rank is -4, then the repair rate is doubled to 3000 rep per hour.
    - However, it now properly repairs only if your reputation is at least rank -4 or higher rather than whenever you have negative reputation. 
    - While rank -4 or lower is considered war, rank -4 has traditionally allowed the player to begin repairing reputation other than via bloodshed. This is consistent with vanilla reputation mechanics.
  - Rationale:
    - Missions give significantly more rep and can still be passively done by using fleets.
    - As the benefit costs a perk slot and NPC factions evaluate the status quo every 8 hours, the reputation gain has been significantly increased so that the passive reputation repair is meaningful as a secondary source of reputation repair rather than negligible under the old system.
