7.  A. Research has been adjusted to provide more research points if you are researching ships of your own race.
	B. Research points have also been increased if your research station borders an NPC sector of your own race.
	This is more realistic, given the availability and proximity of research data, ship parts, etc. within your own race.
	Overall default Research Station output has been lowered by 30%. (player adjustable value in 9972-L044.xml)
	You can still research other race ships, but it will be slower than before and you won't get the juicy bonus's.
	
	Prior to this patch, Bonus research per cycle points were fixed at +3 for both of the bonus's (A & B) listed above.  The new bonus
	values are now a percentage of the base research points for that sector, meaning the higher the research stat for the sector,
	the higher these bonus values will be.
	
	The combined effect of this is a large bonus to researching ships of your own Race and more bonus for doing it near your race allies.
