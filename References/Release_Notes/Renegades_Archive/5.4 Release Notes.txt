. Added a new Outpost Population Transfer ability to the Outpost Manager.
	Accessed from the Hangar screen of the outpost, you select a destination outpost and this will automatically
	transfer 5 population from that outpost to a destination outpost every 10 minutes until you either stop
	it or the destination outpost is full.  This can help you maximize a critical outposts population quickly using 'feeder'
	outposts for population supply.

. Fixed exploit where the Player could build a Yaki ship while having Infamy and Recycle it when they don't have Infamy,
	which essentially gives the player free population.  Now, when Yaki ships are recycled, the player will always get

. Reverse engineering no longer produces an exact 75% progress for that ship's blueprint for one single ship recycling.
	To compensate slightly for this, the maxiumum research progress you can obtain through reverse engineering has been raised to 85%.
	Research gained through reverse engineering has been changed to a tiered system with diminishing returns.  
	Each time you learn a little more, but less each time.	Additionally, partial reverse engineering of 'Advanced' ship blueprints is now possible.
	
	* Current Progress %            Progress % to add when ship is R.E.
	*     0 -25                            +40%
	*     26-50                            +25%
	*     51-85                            +10%
	
	Maximum research through reverse engineering is now 85%, or 50% if an advanced ship.  
	The final % research to obtain the blueprint must be accomplished through standard research.
	Due to rounding errors on smaller project sizes, these Progress % additions can be slightly lower than listed.  This mostly effects
	small ships such as M3-M5's.
