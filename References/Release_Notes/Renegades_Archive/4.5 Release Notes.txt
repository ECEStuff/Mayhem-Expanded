-------------- Covert Operations ---------------------

This features allows an installed "Software Signature Disruptor" module to effectively allow that player owned
ship to appear as a Pirate to the A.I. and further severely limits all A.I. commonwealth patrols from classifying it as a hostile,
even if you are at war with them.

Doing so allows this ship to travel where a Pirate ship might freely travel and 'mostly' cloaks your ship in enemy owned space.
This allows the player to setup covert Looters, or even Traders to move goods through hostile space.  Detection by Pirates is
prevented and detection by hostile commonwealth patrol ships is extremely limited.
You can also use this to setup tactical situations where you bring your own ships into an enemy held system without being immediately attacked,
allowing for excellent scouting, or even a devastating first strike capability.

	. This feature does NOT allow such a ship to dock where it would not normally be allowed to dock.
	. Ships so equipped will reveal themselves, at least temporarily, if they attack someone.
	. Police will not scan a ship with a Software Signature Disruptor installed. (!fight.defend.monitor.xml)
	. Ships so equipped may still be attacked by the A.I. given they appear as Pirates, but with greatly reduced chance.
	. This module is found in the Upgrade Ship menu, and costs 20 Blue Crystals, so the upgrade is expensive.  
		Blue Crystals are also a scarce resource at trade stations, so it will be important
		to either enlist your own automated mining ships, or to use the Crystal Transmuter to upgrade lower valued crystals.
	. Only police, border patrol, and rearguard A.I. fleets attack pirates.  BattleGroups and Task Forces and escorts do not.

. Limit player Trade agents to Profitable Trades only.
	Until now, the vast majority of NPC to NPC Trades being made by Player owned Trade ships would result in 0 profit.  Yes, ZERO.  No check
	was in place to ensure the buy price was higher than the sell price and so there was a lot of wasted time on trades that did nothing but
	benefit NPC logistics at the expense of YOUR traders.  This is no longer the case.
	Player owned Trade workers, that have completed reviewing their homebase's outpost trade needs will seek work trading between NPC's.
	This has not changed.  However, now they will only accept trades that make a profit.  Prior to this, 9/10 of these trades made no profit at all.
	Trading to our from your own outposts will not change in terms of prices.
	Note, that your Trade Rank affects how much profit you make doing Trade Missions for NPC's.  This rank is boosted for you ONLY by profits you 
	make in your trades.  So this change will be helpful in ensuring the trades you make at least this way also help your Trade Rank.

.  Logistics / Ship Repairs
	I have added the ability to fit a ship with Repair lasers and use this as a repair vessel.  The Private Corp. Truelight Seeker has been 
	designated for this role, as it has no general usage in Mayhem 3 and is a vessel you can already research.  This is an M6 class vessel,
	and is originally of Goner design.  As peaceful galactic citizens, the Goners were the only race to develop a vessel capable of utilizing
	repair lasers.  This ship is very slow, has minimal armor and reduced mobility.  Tt's focus is repair, not combat, so you will need to
	escort it.  The primary benefits of this ship are that it provides up to 6 repair lasers, making repair work far faster than using a
	single laser from your spacesuit.  Also, given you are in a ship, you are far less vulnerable.  Automatic repairs are not yet implemented,
	so at present, you will have to pilot the ship and shoot the target manually with the repair lasers.

.  Greatly enhanced the use of Missiles, making them far more impactful.

	Fixed an old condition where Ships were being instructed to never fire their missiles if the single hit would do enough damage to
	destroy the target.  This was being done to conserve missiles in the hope that 'someone' would finish off the target
	using lasers.

	This would often result in ships such as the [M8] sitting there doing nothing but Dying, while they had perfectly good
	Fox class anti-figher missiles installed.  They simply wouldn't fire because one missile does enough damage to kill a fighter.
	You would also see ships such as the [M8] just sitting there watching the stars twinkle while a heavily damaged enemy large
	ship is nearly dead, but alive enough to keep killing.  Again.. one missile would have killed it and so it chose not to fire
	to conserve that missile.
	
	This has been removed.  Now, it will fire that missile, provided it is within 80% of the max range of the missile
	being used.  Often, a Missile was being fired at 100% range and the target was going the other way and the missile
	simply ran out of fuel.  Now, it will fire only within the 80% envelope of the missile's max range.
	
	Also, Artilliary missiles will not fire on anything but Large or Huge Ships, along with Stations.
