.  Added a new "Deploy all stations to" command for TL's.   This will deploy ALL station kits it has in it's cargo to a sector automatically.
	If there is insufficient Sector Support in the target system for all the stations the TL is carrying, it will deploy as many as it can.
	This command does NOT support automatic deployment of Outposts or Mines:  The command will be rejected if any are in the cargo.
	If the target system is unclaimed, this will conquer the sector	when the first station is deployed.
	After deployment, the TL will return home.
	This command will not be available to TL's that are assigned as the global Architect.

.  Protectorates have been removed.

	The protectorate system is a misnomer.  This term means that one nation is protecting another.  
	This is not at all what happens in Mayhem.  In Mayhem, this is simply a global flag on a system that says "This sector was just conquered or tranferred, and is invulnerable for 6 hours".  It was meant to slow the game down, but artificial roadblocks to the progression of war IMO are not a good thing.
	

	1. The A.I. magically materializes free Outposts in UN-OWNED sectors, and often	behind your lines.
		You can't just clear out a system and bypass it and keep pressing forward in a scortched earth rampage.  
		Currently, you have to actually take ownership of any system you clear out as you press forward or risk a magical enemy outpost popping
		in there from thin air.  It's important to note that the A.I. doesn't need a TL to haul an outpost kit like the player does.  It cheats.
		I don't like collecting 30+ systems as I take over an area.  I don't want to manage them, I just want to deny them to the enemy.
		
		- Renegades already has something in place to prevent a recently "stripped" system (no stations at all) from being claimed by the A.I. for a period of time.  See ID 123 in the 9973 t-file for time duration customization on this.  This is eliminates problem 1 above.  A protectorate status doesn't add any value at all.
		
		
	2.	When the A.I. declares war on you, you can't simply go attack any enemy sector you want to.  Often you will find many enemy sectors are still 
		under a protectorate timer for some "other" reason and the player's hands are tied.  Even after Truces are made between
		other nations, the protectorates continue to exist for up to 6 hours.	If the player kills a station in a protectorate 
		system, they are fined by "terracorp", which is not even an agency capable of enforcement and has no business getting into
		your business.  There's nothing worse than to have the A.I. declare war on you, only for you to be artificially restricted in what 
		you can attack to defend yourself.
		
		- For problem 2 above, the protectorate serves no good purpose whatsoever for the player.

The following changes were from betelguese97
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Removed reputation, favor, maintenance, hacking time cost, +1 maximum perk, and EMP cooldown from terraforming effects list.
  - These made no sense from terraforming. Terraforming is about changing the planet, and these effects don't have any geographical analogs.
- Removed weaker version of terraforming effects.
- Terraforming effects are divided into two tiers: common (90% chance to get) and epic (10% chance).
  - All existing effects have been moved to the common category. Epic effects are equivalent to 2 common effects.
- Reworked improved version of terraforming effects.
  - Now called the epic tier.
  - Added "population+", "research+", "support+", "repair+", "industry", and "specialization+" effects. They give:
    - Population+: +30 pops and +3 pop growth rate
    - Research+: +60% research rate
    - Support+: +4 station support
    - Repair+: +100% repair rate and +2 station support
    - Industry: +50% more food, e-cell, waste, and tax.
    - Specialization+: +5% max specialization
- Reworked existing versions of terraforming effects
  - Now moved to common tier.
  - Changed population, research, support, and specialization effects.
    - No longer gives a random amount (20-40 pops, 30-50% research rate, 3-4 station support).
    - Now gives +20 pops, +30% research, +2 station support, and +3% max specialization respectively.
- Separated increased tax, e-cells, waste, and food into distinct categories. Previously, they were grouped under one category before selecting one of the four.
- Pop growth rate from terraforming effect now applies to initial population growth as well.
- Duplicate effects are allowed. Thus, it is theoretically possible for a sector to get 2 standard population effects.
