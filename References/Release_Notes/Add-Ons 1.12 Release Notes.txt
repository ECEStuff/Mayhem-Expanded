-----------------------------------------
		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Changed attributes for the following ships and lasers:
  - Proteus Raider
    - Cargo capacity increased: 1650 -> 2150
    - Marine capacity increased: 15 -> 20
    - The cargo capacity is for parity with the regular Proteus. 
    - As Paranid lack a boarding solution (no TP+ or special boarding ships), this ship gets 5 extra marines to help with boarding.
  - Blastclaw
    - Cargo capacity increased: 210 -> 260
    - Marine capacity increased: 0 -> 5
    - This gives the blastclaw a role as a fast courier and boarding ship, as it lacks firepower for an M3+.
  - Spitfyre
    - Now a neutral ship; was previously Argon.
    - Can mount ALL fighter class weapons, Alpha Kyon Emitter, and GRC.
    - Container class reduced: L -> M
    - This makes it a jack of all trades, master of none. For example, the GRC allows it to hit targets more accurately from afar, while it still has heavy-hitting fighter weapons like the EMPC. 
    - Its versatility means it can equip any fighter weapon that the player has found and fill in roles that other factions' fighters lack.
  - Lasers
    - GRC
      - Reduced container class: L -> M
      - Decreased relvalue: 56 -> 42
    - M/AML
      - Container class increased: M -> L
    - Kyon Emitters (OCV weapons)
      - Alpha: Decreased relvalue: 36 -> 12
      - Beta: Decreased relvalue: 96 -> 72
      - Kyon emitters are quirky. Their costs are abnormally high relative to the stats they provide. The Alpha behaves more like standard fighter lasers while the Beta is excellent for burst damage. The Alpha was made significantly cheaper as it's equipped only on the #deca_deaf/fade ships by default, and it's worse than the other standard fighter weapons. The Beta is made a bit cheaper so that its cost justifies its low DPS for an M6 weapon and high DPS for a fighter weapon. The Gamma is okay as is.

- Spitfyre
  - No longer spawns with Argon NPC fleets.
  - Player-made Spitfyres no longer spawn with free lasers.
  - Research cost is now tripled.
  - Rationale:
    - Like other Private Corp ships, this ship has some quirks. In this case, it is the only ship which is fully customizable, but it requires the player to fully outfit them manually or via templates.
    - The other drawbacks mean that it cannot be scanned; it must be researched in order to be acquired. The research cost is about as much as a ship variant - not too steep but not as readily available as other fighters.
