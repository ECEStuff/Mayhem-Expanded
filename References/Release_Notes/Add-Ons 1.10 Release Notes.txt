Updating infrastructure once again. The mod has been updated to 1.10.

Previous savegames compatibility: PARTIAL.
- A new game is not required but recommended if you fly your own Kha'ak ships. 
  - This is because the cockpit fix (see fixes section) does not apply to existing ships.

Previous galaxies compatibility: YES.

-----------------------------------------
		ADDITIONS
-----------------------------------------
- New perks: Supply depot, Assimilator, and Droid factory
  - Supply depot: the Outpost provides +50% supply. Mutually exclusive with Dry dock.
  - Assimilator: Must be allied with the OCV. The Outpost gains +20% more population from recycling non-Terraformer ships. 
    - Mutually exclusive with Droid factory.
  - Droid factory: Must be allied with the OCV. The Outpost assembles up to 5 pops per consumption cycle (default: 10 minutes). 
    - Pop assembly stops when the Outpost has 75% of its restricted maximum or absolute maximum population, whichever is lower.
    - Costs 40 hull plating per pop assembled.
    - Mutually exclusive with Assimilator.

		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Updated Population stability perk: now mentions that it removes the innate population growth penalty but not the penalty from the tax perk.

	. Mayhem.Perk.SetInfo.xml

- Updated pirates claiming abandoned ships:
  - Pirates can no longer claim ships from within any range. Now, they must be within 10km of an abandoned ship.
  - Reduced chance from 50% to 20%.
  - These changes give parity with tugs claiming ships - tugs have to be near a ship to claim.

	. Mayhem.Task.Pirate.Harassment.xml

- Tax changes:
  - Removed tax efficiency reduction (was causing issues with tax efficiency growth).
  - Instead of 1/3 tax efficiency, tax income is now directly reduced to 1/3 as much (534 per pop -> 178 per pop). 
    - This is effectively the same intended outcome.
  - Changed tax perk: now guarantees 300% tax efficiency instead of 100% (was too easy to get 100%).
