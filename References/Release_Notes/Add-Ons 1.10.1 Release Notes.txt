-----------------------------------------
		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Marine leveling:
  - Random skill gain bonus reduced from 0-10 to 0-5.
  - Narrowed number of marines to level up:
    - Hacking: 1-3 -> 2-3
    - Mechanical: 4-8 -> 5-7
    - Engineering: 4-10 -> 6-8
    - Fighting is unchanged.
  - Marine XP gain is now penalized by 5% per casualty during the post-boarding operation.
    - This does not apply to the first fighting level up for recruits.
    - For example: if there are 11 marines when the ship is hacked and 2 die, then the surviving 9 marines gain 90% as much XP rather than 100%.
    - This change addresses an exploit where the player could sacrifice recruit marines to easily level up veterans, but it rewards boarding operations that minimize losses.
  - Reduced mechanical XP gain by 20% and engineering XP gain by 15%.
    - After testing with both fresh marines and crimson commando marines (hired from the black market), I found that marines were leveling up these skills quickly but not as fast as fighting or hacking. Mechanical was the fastest.

	. ZH.Sig.Captured.xml

- OCV plot now selects sectors based on scoring.
  - If the player has Outposts, it checks the total specialization (ship + station + missile + lasers) and picks the Outpost with the least overall specialization.
  - If the player doesn't have any Outposts, it evaluates the sector's overall score based on population, station, and research limits. The sector with the lowest score is selected.
    - For reference, the average stats are 95 pops, 10 station slots, and 70% research. The weights are 40%, 35%, and 25% respectively.
