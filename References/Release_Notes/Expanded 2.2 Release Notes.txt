The mod has been updated to 2.2. Starting with 2.2, Add-Ons has been rebranded as Mayhem Expanded.

Previous savegames compatibility: NO. 
Previous galaxies compatibility: NO*.

*Notes
- A new galaxy is required for support with the new map generator.
- A new game is also required because existing saves may contain stale instances of the previous !fight.attack.object, which can persist on running combat tasks and may still trigger the old crash.
- ZMap has been replaced with Azimuth for these reasons:
  - To provide a source-available map generator
  - No hidden penalties for playing on other editions. Azimuth works consistently regardless of Mayhem edition.
  - Improved UIs and generation: larger, more organized UIs and more realistic generation.
  - Changed green and red colored text to dark blue and dark red (accessibility).
  - Now removes the legacy 00044.pck and legacy Mayhem Galaxy Generator.
  - Support for new game starts. For example, prior to Expanded 2.2, Xenon Defect could start in a Terran sector by selecting your start location in Terran space.
  - Capitalization and naming inconsistencies in the Universe Map info.
    - For example: Dejure -> De Jure, Terran memory -> Terran Memory.

-----------------------------------------
- Rebalancing starting conditions:
  - Selecting the wealth ascension no longer grants +500 Teladi reputation (nice Easter Egg, but this was giving a benefit).
  - Only your starting ship's blueprint is automatically unlocked at the start of the game. On Insane difficulty, it is not automatically unlocked.
    - Blueprints for the Galleon, TLS, and from starting bonus ships no longer have their blueprints automatically unlocked.
  - Bonus ships:
    - TPs selected as a bonus ship no longer come with a Docking Computer.
    - Starting without a bonus ship now installs 1 Pandora Tuning on your starting ship.
    - All bonus ships, including those from Quickstart, now spawn at random locations in your starting sector.
      - This is to ensure that the ships always spawn in your starting sector, not necessarily where you are currently at.
  - Starting credits is now always 20k on Easy, Normal, and Hard. The starting credits question has been removed.
  - Up to 6 random sectors 2 jumps away are now always revealed. The sector reveal question has been removed.
  - Quickstart changes:
    - OCV no longer start building with halved production rate.
    - Xenon Defect now receives Phanon M3 and TS ships instead of Xenon ships. 
      - The player's initial M4 remains the Xenon M. This is intentional.
    - Decreased starting money: 2M -> 0. The starting credits, early tax, early fleet, and tempo compensate.
    - Decreased starting fleet: 12 M3, 5 TS, and 2 M6 -> 3 M3 and 2 TS.
      - Starting TSes no longer contain additional cargo.
      - Starting bonus ships are now standardized instead of randomized for consistency across gamestarts.
        - M3 bonuses now provide Spitfyres. The bonus Spitfyres cannot be scanned for their blueprint, but they can still be recycled for research.
    - Starting reputation changed from +3k with all factions, in addition to +1k with the origin faction, to rank 6 with only the origin faction.
  - Changed TS ascension requirement to freighters (TS/TP). In addition to TS, TP now count toward the goal.
  - Changed Pirate (fame) and Xenon requirement (power). Yaki and OCV now count toward these respective requirements.
    - These are harder factions and fall under the same categories (outlaw, terraformer).

  - Quickstart was intended to skip the early Ascension phase, but its previous rewards gave it both a substantial tempo advantage and significantly greater guaranteed starting value.
  - These changes refocus Quickstart on tempo: it trades starting wealth for immediate tempo and a minimum viable fleet, while Ascension delays sector expansion in exchange for a substantially larger credit reward.
  - Starting choices have similarly been cleaned up for consistency between choices and to provide initial assets without unnecessarily bypassing normal progression.
  - Xenon Defect receives non-reproducible Phanon ships as limited early support while retaining the Xenon M as its initial ship and keeping Phanon ship production gated behind plot progression.
  - The Spitfyre was chosen to standardize combat effectiveness across Quickstart, regardless of origin faction. However, it is scan-locked so that it doesn't bypass research progression.

- Configuration
  - The game now validates the stat modifier and galaxy start type selected from Azimuth.
    - Stat modifiers: Extra Stats and Saboteur.
  - Difficulty modifiers are now locked for each save once the starting questions are completed.
    - A summary of effective settings, including mandatory difficulty overrides, is sent after setup and recorded in the message log.

    - Later t-file edits to these modifiers will not affect that campaign.
    - This keeps campaign rules consistent, making modifiers lasting conditions to plan around.
  - All bailed ships, including capitals, now respect the global bailed ship cap (80).
  - Station loot drop configurable has been deprecated to prevent loot pinatas from stations.
  - Mobile Factory efficiency is now standardized at 50% for consistency between Nomad and player mobile factories.
  - Research pacing parameters, including the Cyberwar Center cycle time, are now standardized and are no longer configurable.
    - This prevents invalid configurations and ensures research mechanics and bonuses remain balanced around a consistent baseline.
  - Increased overextension limit (player's fleet power per sector): 150 fleet power per sector -> 333 fleet power per sector
    - As a rule of thumb, approximately 70% of the player's supply goes to military. 
    - 150 was too easy to fulfill with just several fighters. 333 is around 70% supply (on Insane) generated by 95 pops, the average population per sector.
  - Quantum Tube trades at Trading Stations have been removed due to bypassing shipbuilding logistics and violating Trading Station principles.
    - Trading Stations trade only intermediate wares.
  - Decreased Nomad Flagship loot threshold while looting Xenon sectors: 200 wares -> 100 wares (no longer configurable).
  - Multiplier variables (OCV cycle multiplier, pirate population multiplier) have been deprecated.
  - Faction revival during the Xenon Crisis is now always enabled.
  - Maximum number of Pirate Bases now scales with galaxy size. The minimum is still 12.
  - The missile spam performance flag (variable 129 in the 9973 t-file) now applies only to non-missile boats.
    - Previously, this applied to non-M7 missile boats. However, missile boats' primary DPS comes from missiles, and M7s are not the only missile boats (M1 and M2 missile boats exist).
  - OWP construction is now always enabled.
  - Removed the adjustable looter range parameter. Looters now always use their homebase trade range. 
    - This prevents the player from manipulating the AI's looters.
  - Removed the no bail and player bailing flags, as bailing could be used as a means for the player to cheat death.
  - Hard and Insane now enable Pirate Base spawning in player sectors.

- Mobile Factories
  - Mobile Factory Loop Production has been reworked to improve production reliability and cargo handling.
   - Player mobile factories now produce 1 complete cycle at a time rather than in bulk. This gives the player more control.
   - Nomad factories still process resources in bulk because they're autonomous.
  - Mobile factories now check available cargo space before starting production and wait if the completed products would not fit.
  - Completed production is now held until the entire batch can fit, preventing partial production if cargo space changes during production.
  - Improved synchronization between Nomad Traders and Nomad Mobile Factories.
    - Nomad Traders will no longer deliver additional cargo while a completed production batch is waiting for cargo space.
  - Ships now require the Mobile Factory Kit to start or continue production, preventing production without the required equipment.
  - Accessing the Mobile Factory menu no longer requires the ship to be in space.
    - This was a legacy restriction from when Mobile Factory single production ran as the ship's primary task.

- Missions
  - Reworked mission generation to reduce savescumming.
    - Mission types and difficulties are now predetermined using separate caches for each mission-giving faction, rather than randomized when missions are generated.
  - Improved Rescue mission: ship now spawns with 0.5% hull (was 200-1000 hull). Now checks crew status: all crew must be alive and in the designated ship in order to complete the mission.
  - Reworked Provision mission: now distributes requested resources to mission giver's assets.
  - Reworked Suppression mission due to spawning artificial military forces.
    - Suppression missions now appear only when the mission giver is hostile or in Total War with another Commonwealth faction that still owns sectors. Yaki may offer these missions but cannot be selected as targets. Phanon neither offers nor serves as a target for Suppression missions.
    - Now tasks the player to eliminate a Border Patrol, Battle Group, Rearguard, or Task Force leader.
    - The mission fails if a different faction eliminates the target. Elimination means the target was destroyed or changed ownership.
    - Rewards vary depending on the leader's ship value. Tougher targets generally award more credits, reputation, and Loyalty.
  - Added cleanup and post-mission handling for Suppression, Rescue, Police, Delivery, Patrol, Bait, and Defense missions.
    - Defense missions now fail if the station is destroyed by the Xenon or the player.
    - Mission-generated Xenon and Kha'ak ships now despawn when their mission is aborted, preventing players from deliberately spawning hostile forces to weaken NPC factions.
      - Exception: Pirates spawned by Delivery missions now transition into regular Pirate harassment after the mission ends.
    - Potential ship rewards are reclaimed when appropriate, preventing players from deliberately aborting missions to steal them.
      - Ships currently flown by the player are not destroyed to avoid forcing a game over; the increased failure penalty applies instead.
      - Delivery ships are retained if a destination station was destroyed, since the resulting failure may be outside the player's control.
      - The Police mission's stolen ship is retained because it is an existing worker rather than a mission-generated reward ship.

  - Mission failure now displays the penalty. The penalties are -500 reputation and -1 Loyalty by default.
    - Reputation penalty scales by difficulty:
      - Easy/Normal: 1x
      - Hard: 1.5x
      - Insane: 2x
    - Certain player-responsible failures double both penalties:
      - Bait or Rescue: the player is personally flying the claimed mission ship.
      - Police: the culprit has been identified and the stolen ship claimed.
      - Delivery: all destination stations remain intact and the player is personally flying the delivery ship.
      - Supplies: always.
    - Note: Loyalty cannot fall below 0, and the failure notification reports only Loyalty actually lost.

  - Improved Bait missions. Kha'ak now target the bait ship first, then the player's current ship, then defend the sector.
  - Improved Intel missions. Can now be completed if the scan is 100% even after the target station is destroyed.
  - Patrol missions can no longer be triggered by fleet leaders. Only the player's personal ship can trigger Xenon spawns.
  - Mission Board is now sorted by earliest expiration time.
  - Mission generator now properly generates 0-2 missions per sector instead of filling up to the 2-mission cap.
  - Mission generation chance increased from 17% to 25% to compensate for the lower mission density.
  - Mission timer is now randomized between 50-70 minutes rather than between 60-70 minutes.
  - Improved station selection and retry handling.
  - Improved message completion and failure pop-up messages.
  - Improved mission dialogue messages.
  - Insane difficulty now adds the limited mission pool once instead of twice when weighting mission selection.
    - The reworked mission generation system already reduces overall mission availability, so the previous weighting made ship-reward missions excessively rare.
  - Xenon and Kha'ak carriers (ships with hangars) from Defense and Bait missions now come with support ships.
    - These support ships do not give mission rewards upon destruction.
    - Added Kha'ak Carrier to Bait missions.
  - Xenon, Kha'ak, and Pirate groups from Xenon patrol, Kha'ak Bait, and Delivery missions respectively now use more dynamic 3D spawn positions.
    - Previously, Xenon patrols always spawned below y=0, while Kha'ak and Pirate groups used a small set of fixed spawn positions.
    - This increases positional variety and makes hostile mission spawns less predictable.

  - Fixed infected, non-OCV made ships being cured by boarding them back after an OCV takeover.
  - Fixed player fleets being disbanded if the convertee is a fleet leader. Fleets are now preserved by passing leadership to a clean follower.

    - Converted ships are now properly re-equipped with lasers and shields. Hull is also restored to at least 65%.
    - Converted ships also receive standard OCV defenses: HPD, ISL, plus a chance for AFS (50% for capital ships, 25% otherwise) if not already installed.
  - OCV-built ships now consistently participate in virus takeovers regardless of their current owner.
    - OCV-aligned players can use takeovers to mobilize nearby OCV forces, including OCV ships currently controlled by the Xenon.
  - Converted ships are properly reorganized into active invasion groups after takeover.
    - Converted fighters become support ships for OCV carriers.
    - Ships unsuitable for combat (TS, TP, TL, Truelight Seeker, and Aran) now transform into OCV ships.
  - Fixed OCV infection timers resetting when infected ships were copied or replaced.
    - Replacement ships now inherit the original infection deadline rather than receiving a newly randomized incubation period.
    - Infection deadlines also persist across virus-task restarts.
    - Pending OCV takeovers are preserved during ship replacement, preventing matured infections from being delayed or reset.

  - Previously, having the player inside an infected ship when its timer expired could terminate the virus task and effectively cure the ship.
  - Infected player ships now remain infected and convert after the player leaves them.
  - Broadcast takeovers no longer convert or interrupt the current player ship; its own infection remains pending.
  - Player-facing ship-loss counts, sacrifice progress, and reputation penalties now count only player-owned ships actually converted.
