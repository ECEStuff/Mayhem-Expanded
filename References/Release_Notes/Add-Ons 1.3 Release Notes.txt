1. Starting Conditions
  - Only 1 free sector at the start. Upon conquering your first sector, all unknown sectors fall to Commonwealth control.
  - You now become a nation when you get at least 1 sector rather than when you get at least 8 (default value) sectors or when you get 1/4 of the usual power milestone (default: 2000).
  - You no longer start with any free items (no free ships, blueprints, money, revealed sectors).
  - Ascension
    - Quickstart ascension is disabled. 
    - Get a special ascension which has higher quotas (33-50% higher) and randomized tasks. 
    - 3 tasks are pulled from 11 tasks (removed a duplicate task that asks you to have money) taken from normal paths.
    - Quota Exceptions

      - Get credits: 500k (unchanged)

3. NPC behavior
- Xenon, OCV, Kha'ak, and Renegades are tougher.
  - Increased spawns by 25-50% (missions already have scaled spawns, so they're not mentioned here).
  - Renegade defenders spawn with tougher ships.
  - Now a 50% chance to have a 3rd Xenon station.
  - Xenon patrols are reduced if you join the OCV.
- Xenon jump beacon infections
  - Time needed to infect your beacon is reduced by 50%.
  - Takes only 3 beacons (rather than 4 on hard) to reach the maximum chance of infection.
- NPC Outposts have a 40% chance to have the Pandora Tuning perk. Renegade ships can now spawn with Pandora tuning.
- NPC factions are more aggressive.
  - Explorers have a 1% chance to conquer unknown sectors (compared to 0.2% on hard).
  - NPC factions can declare war without a shared border.
  - Raiders have a 75% chance to prioritize the player, compared to 50% on hard.
- NPC capships are twice as likely to spawn with internal defenses (25% for firewall, 66% chance for ISL) compared to hard mode.
- Bailed ship chances are reduced, and bailed ships retain 25% of lasers (compared to 50% on hard).
