MAYHEM EXPANDED 2.2
===================

Renegades Add-Ons is now Mayhem Expanded. Version 2.2 introduces Azimuth,
reworks campaign progression and fleet behavior, and fixes combat,
mission, economy, and Terraformer Virus issues.

COMPATIBILITY AND INSTALLATION
------------------------------
- Existing saves: NOT compatible. Start a new game.
- Existing galaxies: NOT compatible. Generate a new galaxy with Azimuth.
- Follow the updated README, install the complete release, and run the
  included cleanup.bat before generating and activating your new galaxy.
- Back up your installation and saves before updating. Do not restore old
  generated galaxy files or ship and weapon overrides over the update.

A new game is required because existing saves can retain older combat
tasks, including !fight.attack.object, and remain exposed to the old
crash. A new galaxy is required for the revised generation and metadata.

HIGHLIGHTS
----------
- Azimuth replaces ZMap, with revised galaxy generation, editing,
  validation, configuration tools, and ship and weapon randomization.
- Rebalanced starting rewards, Quickstart, and Ascension requirements.
- Reworked research, taxes, supply, Stock Exchanges, and terraforming.
- Missions use predetermined selection and target existing fleets where
  appropriate, with clearer completion, failure, and cleanup rules.
- Reworked Xenon garrisons, OCV production, and Kha'ak raids.
- Standardized ship variants and revised ship and laser balance.
- Fixed fleet leadership, targeting, repairs, and OCV virus takeovers.

1. AZIMUTH MAP GENERATOR
-----------------------
Azimuth replaces ZMap as Expanded's map generator. It is source-available
and applies selected options consistently across supported Mayhem editions.
Azimuth will never introduce edition-specific penalties or artificial
feature restrictions. Explicitly selected gameplay modifiers remain part
of galaxy customization.

- Reworked territory generation around the gate network, with connected
  faction territories, separated faction starts, and Unknown frontiers.
- Reworked Xenon allocation and enclave placement. Chaotic galaxies use
  clustered Xenon and require at least two Unknown enclaves; other starts
  retain the four-Unknown-enclave requirement.
- Improved gate regeneration, generation retries, and log scrolling.
- Added editing checks for ownership, sector addition and removal, de jure
  ownership, company assignment, Xenon counts, and sector stat limits.
- Separated galaxy-start choices from sector stat modifiers and show the
  generated or loaded galaxy's modifier as a read-only summary.
- Added Xenon percentage adjustment and grouped sliders separately from
  other map options.
- Expanded and reorganized interfaces, including Save/Load Galaxy, and
  added tooltips. Improved text colors and use of system background colors.
- Improved the configuration editor and its setting descriptions.
- Improved PNG export and sector information, including planet counts.
- Corrected Xenon Defect starting-sector selection and clarified the
  optional Gamestart Sector and Maelstrom overrides.
- Standardized map terminology, including De Jure and Terran Memory.
- Handles legacy 00044.pck and generator cleanup during activation.
- Revised ship randomization to share attribute modifiers across each
  race and normalize shield capacity with the shield-generator modifier.
  Lower shield tiers are used where permitted to represent adjustments.
- Ship production values use a weighted mean: 40% movement, 40% durability,
  and 20% cargo, limiting economic swings while preserving racial variety.
- Weapon randomization uses racial groups. Repair Lasers and Mobile
  Drilling Systems are excluded, as is the unused Mounted Railgun.
- Laser production values use the mean of average damage, firing rate,
  and range multipliers.
- Uses azimuth_backups for the relevant backups, with readable timestamps.

Galaxy stat modifiers in Expanded
---------------------------------
- Normal: standard sector attributes, 20% base maximum specialization,
  20,000 progress per specialization percentage point, and four base perk
  slots.
- Saboteur: population, research, and station support reduced by 20%;
  30% base maximum specialization, 25,000 progress per percentage point,
  and five base perk slots. NPC tall needs increase by 60% for normalization.
- Extra Stats: population, research, and station support increased by 20%;
  supply and maximum tax efficiency reduced by 20%; base maximum
  specialization reduced to 10%; and terraforming scaling reduced by
  10 percentage points for generic, Gaia, and Barren effects.
- Base specialization changes apply to NPC Outposts as well as player
  Outposts. Difficulty adjustments to perk slots still apply.

These are explicit gameplay choices. Validation checks the imported
galaxy against its declared rules; it does not regenerate sector attributes
or ownership when a fallback is applied.

2. STARTING CONDITIONS AND GALAXY RULES
---------------------------------------

Starting rewards and Ascension
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reworked starting rewards on Easy, Normal, and Hard:
  - Starting credits are fixed at 20,000; the credits question has been removed.
  - Starting exploration is automatic; the sector-reveal question has been removed. The reveal budget scales with galaxy size and is capped at five additional nearby sectors within two jumps, plus the origin sector. See the verification appendix for the origin-sector edge case.
  - Only the initial ship's blueprint is unlocked automatically. Insane does not grant this blueprint.
  - The Galleon, Truelight Seeker, and bonus ships no longer grant automatic blueprints.
  - Bonus TPs no longer include a Docking Computer.
  - Declining a bonus ship installs one Pandora Tuning on the initial ship.
  - All bonus ships, including Quickstart ships, spawn at random positions in the origin sector, even if the player has moved elsewhere.
- Reworked Quickstart:
  - Removed the additional 2 million credits. The ordinary 20,000-credit starting award still applies on eligible difficulties.
  - Reduced the fleet from 12 M3s, 5 TSs, and 2 M6s to 3 M3s and 2 TSs.
  - Starting TSs no longer carry additional cargo.
  - Standardized bonus ships instead of selecting them randomly. Ordinary Quickstart M3 rewards use Spitfyres; these cannot be scanned for blueprints but can be recycled for research.
  - Xenon Defect instead receives Phanon M3s and TSs. The initial player ship remains the Xenon M, and Phanon production remains gated by plot progression.
  - Reputation changes from +3,000 with all factions plus +1,000 with the origin faction to rank 6 with the origin faction only.
  - Removed Quickstart's halved OCV production rate.
  - Quickstart now emphasizes immediate expansion and a smaller initial fleet. Completing Ascension instead delays expansion in exchange for its larger credit reward.
- Updated Ascension:
  - Wealth Ascension no longer grants the hidden +500 Teladi reputation bonus.
  - Freighter requirements now accept both TSs and TPs.
  - Yaki kills count toward Fame's Pirate requirement, and OCV kills count toward Power's Xenon requirement.

Campaign validation and configuration
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Validate the declared galaxy start and sector stat modifier during initialization, before the starting questions.
  - Stat validation and galaxy-start validation are independent.
  - Failed stat validation uses Normal gameplay modifiers; sector attributes remain unchanged.
  - Failed galaxy-start validation uses Standard start rules; sector ownership remains unchanged.
- Lock campaign difficulty modifiers when the starting questions finish.
  - Send a report of effective settings, mandatory difficulty overrides, galaxy start, and stat modifier to the message log.
  - Later t-file edits to these locked modifiers do not change the campaign.
- Standardized or removed the following configuration options:
  - All bailed ships, including capital ships, respect the global cap of 80.
  - Removed the configurable station loot drop option.
  - Mobile Factory efficiency is fixed at 50% for both player and Nomad factories.
  - Research pacing, including the Cyberwar Center cycle time, uses fixed values.
  - Removed the OCV cycle and pirate population multiplier settings.
  - Faction revival during the Xenon Crisis and OWP construction are always enabled.
  - Looters use their homebase's trade range; the separate range setting has been removed.
  - Removed the no-bail and player-bailing flags.
  - Hard and Insane enable Pirate Base spawning in player sectors.
- Increased the overextension threshold from 150 to 333 fleet power per player sector. The new threshold is approximately 70% of the supply generated by 95 population on Insane.
- Removed Quantum Tube trading from Trading Stations to preserve shipbuilding logistics and their intermediate-ware role.
- Reduced the Nomad Flagship's Xenon-sector looting threshold from 200 to 100 wares; it is no longer configurable.
- Pirate Base capacity now scales with galaxy size, with a minimum of 12.
- The missile-spam performance setting, variable 129 in 9973, applies only to non-missile boats. Missile boats remain eligible regardless of ship class.

- Fixed Ascension requirements being determined upon opening the Ascension UI rather than at gamestart.

Initialization fixes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Fixed Jump Point infrastructure variables showing as null during game initialization.
  - The game now initializes a placeholder value before finally computing the cap in ZH.Initialize.
- Fixed factory tutorial being presented shortly after placing an Outpost.
  - This was due to placing temporary player factories to extract mobile factory metadata during initialization.
  - The tutorial is now presented only after initialization has finished.

3. ECONOMY, RESEARCH, AND TERRAFORMING
--------------------------------------

Stock Exchanges
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Expanded Stock Exchanges to the other Commonwealth races and added an industrial outlook indicator.
- Reworked the economic-linkage system initially ported from FoF:
  - Exchanges spawn only in de jure sectors, preventing acquisition through sector purchases.
  - Stock valuations respond to the owner's faction-wide industrial outlook.
  - Outlook considers factory operating conditions and changes in the faction's established industrial scale, using its entire factory base rather than a regional sample.
  - Only the faction's own factories count; player-owned and foreign factories do not directly affect its market.
  - Prices retain random movement but are biased toward valuations supported by the economy.

Research
~~~~~~~~

- Reworked research so Base Research Rate remains relevant throughout the game.
  - Most bonuses scale with Base Research Rate and add to a unified Research Rate.
  - Yaki, Faster Research, and terraforming modifiers remain multiplicative.
  - Faster Research applies after the other research bonuses, affecting their combined output.
- Rebalanced bonuses:
  - Origin faction: +50% -> +35%.
  - Terran origin: replaced +3 Research Points across all projects with +50% Research Rate for Terran ships.
  - Shared border: +33% -> +20%.
  - Matching ship: +35% -> +25%.
  - Coalition: +50 Research Points -> +150% Research Rate.
  - Favor: +20 Research Points -> +70% Research Rate.
  - Relations: +1 Research Point per rank above rank 5 -> +3% Research Rate per rank above rank 5.
- Updated tooltips to distinguish Base Research Rate, bonuses, and final Research Station output.

Supply
~~~~~~

- Reduced supply scaling on Easy, Normal, and Hard:
  - Easy: 3x.
  - Normal: 2x.
  - Hard: 1.6x.
  - Insane: 1x, unchanged.
- Easier difficulties retain more supply, while making the choice between keeping salvaged ships and recycling them more consequential.

Emergency Rations
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Replaced No Food Consumption with Emergency Rations.
  - During shortages, 2 units of produced food replace 1 unit of required food, covering up to 50% of demand.
  - Incompatible with Population Stability and Industrial Overdrive.
  - Removes the self-sustaining food loop available to one-sector empires under the old perk.

Outpost perks and infrastructure
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Removed the +1 perk slot bonus from Easy difficulty.
- Rebalanced Outpost perk slots:
  - Every two permanent wars against Commonwealth factions grant one additional slot per Outpost, down from one per war. Yaki and Phanon remain excluded.
  - Adjacency Perk Bonus requires three adjacent player Outposts to purchase.
  - After accounting for the perk's own slot cost, three qualifying neighbors grant one net slot and four grant two net slots.
  - Only permanent gate connections count. Losing neighbors reduces the bonus without removing the purchased perk.
- Capital Shipyard upgrades preserve stored wares.
- Capital Shipyard costs scale by +1 Outpost, +30 Blue Crystals, and +15 Green Crystals for each difficulty level.
- Changed Jump Hole costs:
  - Crystals: 10 Red, 5 Yellow, and 1 Blue -> 20 Red, 8 Yellow, 5 Blue, 2 Green, and 1 Black.
  - Also requires 10,000 Energy Cells and one Jump Beacon.
  - Duration: 6 -> 8 hours, matching the AI's eight-hour strategy update interval.
- Reworked Orbital Weapons Platforms (OWPs):
  - Construction costs shipbuilding resources and population instead of fixed station materials and one Black Crystal.
  - NPC factions place initial OWPs after stations have spawned, prioritizing their most valuable sectors.
  - NPC factions can build up to two additional OWPs every eight hours.
  - Maximum OWP limits: Easy 6 -> 4; Hard 1 -> 2.

Mobile Factories
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reworked Mobile Factory loop production and cargo handling.
  - Player factories process one complete production cycle at a time; autonomous Nomad factories still process resources in bulk.
  - Factories check cargo space before starting production.
  - If cargo space changes during production, completed output waits until the entire batch fits instead of being transferred partially.
  - Nomad Traders stop delivering additional cargo while a completed batch is waiting for space.
  - A Mobile Factory Kit is required to start or continue production.
- The Mobile Factory menu can now be opened while docked.

- Combustor can now combust Artefacts, Parcel, and VIP.
  - This removes obsolete items from the player's Outposts, if any are stored.

- Food is now produced at a production : consumption ratio of 1.25:1 (was 2:1)
  - This is due to food production exceeding consumption, potentially saturating the food market.

- Space Weed is now produced only if you have infamy and you have not joined the OCV. This ties Space Weed as an illicit good.
  - Production rate per cycle is now as follows:
    - 1-29 infamy: 1 Space Weed.
    - 30-64 infamy: 2 Space Weed.
    - 65-99 infamy: 3 Space Weed.
    - 100+ infamy: 4 Space Weed.

Taxes
~~~~~

- Tax perk:
  - Reverted minimum tax efficiency from 300% to 100%.
  - Now increases the tax efficiency target by 25%.
- Population scaling:
  - Normalized tax per pop for easier calculations (178 credits/pop -> 180 credits/pop).
  - Replaced tiered population tax bonuses with gradual population-dependent tax efficiency scaling.
  - Population now modifies the tax efficiency target instead of directly multiplying tax income.
  - Efficiency modifiers range from 75% at 60 population to 125% at 200 population, reaching a maximum of 200% at 1,000 population.
- Capital Shipyard:
  - Replaced the immediate +50% tax income bonus with a +50% tax efficiency target bonus.
  - Its income benefits now develop gradually as actual efficiency approaches the target.
- Tax efficiency:
  - Tax efficiency is now tracked individually by each Outpost instead of its sector.
  - Reworked efficiency growth and decay to use the same proportional adjustment rate.
  - Each update now closes approximately 10% of the difference between current and target efficiency, regardless of direction.
    - This significantly accelerates efficiency decay, preventing sectors from retaining excessively high tax income long after their economic conditions deteriorate.
  - Decreased station contribution: +3.03% efficiency target per station -> +2% efficiency target per station
  - Tax efficiency, collection timing, and accumulated tax income are preserved when upgrading an Outpost into a Capital Shipyard.
- Interface:
  - Updated the Outpost menu to display current and target tax efficiency.
  - Replaced the previous growth and deterioration indicators with rising, falling, stable, and Tax perk minimum states.
  - Improved messages for sectors that are not yet generating tax income.
- Fixes:
  - Corrected average sector-distance calculations, including jump beacon validation.
  - Improved tax calculations to reduce the risk of integer overflow in highly developed sectors.

Recycling and terraforming
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reduced base recycling factor (50% -> 40%) to ensure specialization and recycling factor do not overlap.
- Reduced maximum number of recyclers (terraforming effect) to 2 within a sector.
- Reduced recycling factor from capital shipyard (+20% -> +15%) to better increase the value of the recycler terraforming effects.
- Reduced the maximum rare terraforming roll chance from 10% to 5%. The draft estimates an overall rare-effect frequency of approximately 4.4%, compared with 6.5% previously; these estimates require verification before publication.

- Fixed dynamic Outpost specialization accumulating incorrectly, a regression introduced in Expanded 1.1.

- Fixed NPC Outposts not recycling lasers and shields from recycled ships.

- Fixed station recycling validation:
  - Moved redundant code from prompt to station recycling.
  - Capital Shipyards are treated as Outposts for recycling restrictions.

- Fixed non-factory stations being classified as factories by using the shared factory classification logic.

- Fixed terraforming remainders being discarded after terraforming a planet.
  - Remainders now carry over, ensuring that the generic bonuses are accurately applied.

- Fixed resource transfer (sector transfer to another faction or end of Phanon branch) transferring factory resources when only products should be transferred.

4. MISSIONS, DIPLOMACY, AND WORKERS
-----------------------------------

Mission generation and behavior
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reworked mission generation:
  - Mission types and difficulties are predetermined in separate caches for each mission-giving faction.
  - Generate 0-2 missions per sector instead of filling every sector to the two-mission cap.
  - Increased generation chance from 17% to 25% to offset lower mission density.
  - Mission timers range from 50-70 minutes instead of 60-70 minutes.
  - Improved station selection, retry handling, dialogue, and completion and failure messages.
  - The Mission Board sorts by earliest expiration.
  - On Insane, the limited mission pool is added once instead of twice when weighting selection, making ship-reward missions less excessively rare.
- Suppression missions target existing fleets instead of generating military forces.
  - The mission giver must be hostile or in Total War with a Commonwealth faction that still owns sectors.
  - Yaki can offer missions but cannot be targets. Phanon neither offers these missions nor serves as a target.
  - Targets are Border Patrol, Battle Group, Rearguard, or Task Force leaders.
  - Destroying the target or changing its ownership counts as elimination. The mission fails if another faction eliminates it.
  - Credits, reputation, and Loyalty rewards depend on the target ship's value.
- Rescue ships spawn with 0.5% hull instead of 200-1,000 hull points. All crew must remain alive and aboard the designated ship for completion.
- Provision missions distribute delivered resources to the mission giver's assets.
- Bait mission Kha'ak target the bait ship first, then the player's current ship, then defend the sector.
- Intel missions can complete after the target station is destroyed if scanning had already reached 100%.
- Only the player's personal ship triggers Patrol mission Xenon spawns; fleet leaders no longer do so.
- Defense missions fail if the defended station is destroyed by the Xenon or the player.
- Defense and Bait mission carriers include support ships. Support ships do not award mission rewards when destroyed. Bait missions can include a Kha'ak Carrier.
- Xenon Patrol, Kha'ak Bait, and Pirate Delivery groups use more varied three-dimensional spawn positions instead of fixed patterns.
- Improved mission cleanup:
  - Mission-generated Xenon and Kha'ak despawn when their missions are aborted.
  - Delivery mission Pirates become ordinary Pirate harassment groups after the mission ends.
  - Potential reward ships are reclaimed when appropriate. Ships personally flown by the player are retained to avoid a forced game over, with increased failure penalties instead.
  - Delivery ships are retained if a destination station was destroyed.
  - The Police mission's stolen ship is retained because it is an existing worker.
- Mission failures display their penalties:
  - Base penalty: 500 reputation and 1 Loyalty.
  - Reputation multiplier: 1x on Easy and Normal, 1.5x on Hard, and 2x on Insane.
  - Both penalties double for Bait or Rescue if the player is flying the claimed mission ship; Police if the culprit was identified and the stolen ship claimed; Delivery if all destination stations remain intact and the player is flying the delivery ship; and Supplies in all cases.
  - Loyalty cannot fall below zero, and notifications report only the Loyalty actually lost.

Additional mission fixes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Fixed Yaki being excluded from mission givers.
- Fixed missions generating fewer missions due to invalid criteria.
- Fixed missions being checked for expiration after already being removed due to a station ownership change.
- Fixed conflicting colored mission entries, which caused the rest of the mission entry to be default colored.
  - All entries are now default colored to allow parts of the entry to be colored (Suppression missions use colored ship names).
  - Superseded entries remain grayed out.

Cargo Hauler and Taxi jobs
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Joining the OCV removes Cargo Hauler and Taxi registrations.
  - Passengers become slaves and can be converted to Energy Cells with the Combustor perk.
  - Cargo Hauler wares, including drugs, can be combusted at one unit per 15 Energy Cells.
- Increased base payments: Cargo Hauler 4,000-6,000 -> 15,000 credits; Taxi 1,500-2,000 -> 3,000 credits.
- Jobs are predetermined rather than rerolled. Timers are fixed at one hour instead of 30-60 minutes.
- Cargo Hauler deliveries require the player to pilot the registered ship.
- Job range scales with reputation, reaching three jumps at rank 7 or higher.
- Cargo Hauler shipment size scales with the registered ship's cargo capacity.
- Payments depend on delivery distance instead of random rolls.
- Taxi registration requires Cargo Lifesupport System; ships already receive it as standard equipment.
- Taxi availability scales with reputation, reaching three passengers at rank 7 or higher.
- Taxi jobs no longer grant reputation. Cargo Hauler deliveries still do; illegal deliveries reward Yaki reputation instead of the station owner's.
- Fixed duplicate Cargo Hauler destinations, partial loading of shipments that could not fit, and repeated Taxi attempts to use invalid destinations.
- Added a notification when attempting delivery without the complete Cargo Hauler shipment.
- Restored contraband penalties, including for Cargo Hauler jobs:
  - Confiscation removes the affected job.
  - Insufficient credits to pay a fine increase the base reputation penalty according to the shortfall.
  - Fine and reputation multipliers: Easy 0.5x, Normal 1x, Hard 1.5x, and Insane 2x.

Workers
~~~~~~~

- Added a worker homebase indicator.
- Improved Traders and the Trade Report Archive, incorporating fixes from FoF:
  - Traders reserve destination cargo space to reduce redundant deliveries.
  - Traders can retry destination trade locks instead of immediately abandoning a trade.
  - If an external destination's price drops below the purchase price, Traders can redirect to another suitable station in the same sector.
  - Outpost Traders no longer trade with Pirate Bases.
  - The archive tracks each active Outpost Trader's NPC-to-NPC trades, realized profit, maintenance, and net profit. Profit is realized only when NPC-purchased wares are sold to NPC stations.
- Improved Miners:
  - Miners unload at their home Outpost, or the nearest Outpost if not assigned to one. Agents handle subsequent distribution.
  - The unloading threshold is 50% cargo capacity instead of 3,000 cargo units, applies to both player and NPC Miners, and is no longer configurable.
  - Miners check the threshold before and after mining debris, as well as after reaching the destination sector.
- Ported and extended FoF's Tug, Looter, and Explorer improvements:
  - NPCs use behavior previously restricted to player workers.
  - Tugs claim the nearest abandoned ship and recalculate the next target after each claim.
  - Explorers avoid hostile sectors more effectively and maintain a sector list from their homebase.
  - TSs and TPs are no longer eligible player Tugs or Explorers. TMs can serve as Tugs.
  - NPC Tugs use M3s and TMs instead of TPs.
- Changed worker allowances:
  - Player factory Explorers: 4 -> 2.
  - NPC factory Explorers: 1-2 -> 2.
  - NPC Agents: 2 -> 3.
  - NPC Traders: 1-2 -> 2.
  - NPC Miners: 1-3 -> 3.
  - NPC Quantum Tube Factories can support two Miners to help supply Ore and Silicon.

Diplomacy and Loyalty
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Display Truce and Non-Aggression Pact durations in the Ministry of War.
- Provide a reason whenever Loyalty is awarded.
- Bailing a coalition ally's ship or a ship belonging to a faction under truce has diplomatic consequences, as destroying or boarding it would.
- Removed random Loyalty awards from reputation gains on Easy and Normal.
- Destroying a Hive Queen in Yaki territory awards one Yaki Favor Point.
- Removed the free Favor Point for honoring a truce.
- Reworked Loyalty rewards for eliminating large ships:
  - Requires a sector with an existing claim.
  - Only Huge Ships qualify, awarding two Loyalty for assisting either the owner or the claimant against the other party's ship.
  - Boarding and hacking count only when assisting the Yaki; O2 hacking does not qualify.
- Claiming abandoned capital ships, except Black Market M7s, incurs the greater of a flat or percentage-based reputation loss:
  - M7: 500 or 1%.
  - M1: 750 or 1.5%; the flat penalty was 700.
  - M2: 1,000 or 2%.
  - Harder difficulties continue to double these losses.
- Priority-target M7s use the assigning faction's color for their asterisk. For example, a blue asterisk identifies an Argon priority target.
  - Hacking is eligible for the Favor Point reward.
  - Targets must be hostile or in Total War with the assigning faction.
  - Targets remain strictly M7s and may be leaders or followers.
- Doubled faction ethos influence on ultimatum chance:
  - Every five positive ethos points add one percentage point; every five negative points subtract one.
  - At reputation rank 9, positive bonuses are 50% larger and negative penalties are halved.
  - At rank 10, a faction always offers an ultimatum when one is possible and a valid sector can be demanded.

Additional diplomacy fixes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Fixed Restriction favor canceling a different faction's claim.
  - A faction can no longer restrict a sector if it has a different faction's claim.
- Fixed Insane difficulty allowing Favor Points to be bought at rank 6.
  - The requirement has been corrected to rank 7 for consistency with close ally rules (rank 7 on Insane or rank 6 on lower difficulties).
- Fixed coalition check occurring before offering truce.
- Fixed a faction influenced by the fairness favor being unable to conquer unclaimed sectors.
- Fixed Space Weed favor potentially unlocking Space Weed trades at variable number of eligible factories. Now unlocks Space Weed trades at 33% of eligible factories or at least 1.
- Fixed a null reference (playtime) when breaking truces. Also fixed truce scripts overwriting races 1 and 2 with strings.
- Fixed player's Non-Aggression Pact being shown only under player statuses in Ministry of War. Now displayed for both the player and the other faction(s).
- Fixed truces not ending coordinated invasions (CIs).
- Moved Coalition and Phanon cost waiver to Mayhem.Favor.GetCost.

Ultimatums
~~~~~~~~~~

- Fixed another sorting bug with ultimatums. The AI now selects the most valuable sector if there are no de jure sectors under player control.
  - Also fixed reputation loss from losing an alliance not applying to the faction that issued the ultimatum.

Infamy
~~~~~~

- Infamy now caps at 1M to protect against overflow.
- Removed 60 infamy cap from destroying neutral TS for consistency with other infamy sources.

Abandoned ships and Black Market
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Initial abandoned ships and Black Market ships spawn with 30-70% hull instead of 40-70%, giving a midpoint of 50%. Rescue ships use 0.5% hull.
- Initial abandoned-ship selection now includes TPs: 20% TP, 40% TS, and 40% M3.
- Private Corp. ships other than the Truelight Seeker can appear in the Black Market and initial abandoned-ship pool.
- Expanded NPC claim protection to initial abandoned ships, mission ships, Black Market ships, and O2-hacked ships. Bailed ships have no protection.
  - Base duration: 2.5 hours on Easy, 2 on Normal, 1.5 on Hard, and 1 on Insane.
  - Random offset: +/-25% of the difficulty's base duration.
  - Final duration also scales with galaxy size.
  - Initial ships receive 12x duration; Black Market, O2-hacked, and mission ships receive 1x.
  - Black Market and mission-spawned abandoned ships use predetermined locations.
- Rebalanced bailing:
  - All Phanon ships can bail, at 20% of the ordinary frequency, preserving their rarity while allowing occasional access before plot rewards.
  - Kha'ak capital ships bail at 20% of the ordinary frequency, accounting for their rarity and greater presence in raids and missions.
  - Teladi capital ships bail 33% more often instead of twice as often; the fighter bonus remains 33%.
- The Black Market allows six purchase slots within a one-hour window. Most ships cost one slot; M7s cost three.
- Purchase options sort by role rather than alphabetically: M7, M6, other combat roles, TM, TS, and TP.
- Consolidated Black Market initialization after the starting questions, reducing initialization delays and handling sector selection in the same place.
- Increased ordinary Black Market prices:
  - M3: 40,000 -> 50,000 credits.
  - M6: 300,000 -> 325,000.
  - TM: 125,000 -> 250,000.
  - TS: 35,000 -> 50,000.
  - TP: 25,000 -> 60,000.
  - Prices account for the availability of ship variants.
- On Insane, TS and TP prices are both 300,000 credits, replacing 350,000 and 250,000 respectively.
- The Crimson Commando arrives with a Hades Prototype and Toucan Prototype instead of a Viper and Magnir, matching the Yaki's M8 and TP+ introduced in Expanded 2.0.

Mercenaries and group leadership
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Reworked mercenary group leadership and recovery:
  - Groups retain their identity after a leader dies and reform from their own members.
  - Fixed mercenary and Xenon/OCV leaders following each other; groups maintain one leader.
  - Mercenary followers monitor changes to their escort target.
  - Hired leaders no longer leave despite a valid player escort target. If no nearby player ship is available, they follow the player's personal ship.
- The Mercenaries interface displays balance and wages every 15 minutes.
- Corrected mercenary leader charges from 20% to the full rate used for the rest of the group.
- Fixed incorrect ship counts in the Mercenaries interface.

5. WARFARE, XENON, OCV, AND KHA'AK
----------------------------------

Initial enemy forces
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Preserved Xenon and OCV defenders in Peaceful and Chaotic galaxies instead of destroying them during initialization.
- Chaotic galaxies use one station per non-portal Xenon sector, regardless of difficulty.
- Increased Hard's initial Xenon and OCV spawn scaling from +20% to +33%.
- Initial Kha'ak and Xenon roamers spawn only in Standard galaxies.

Territory and enemy fleets
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Removed the sector-count grace radius. NPC expansion unlocks when the player completes Ascension, rather than waiting for the player to own a specified number of sectors.
- Removed the minimum Unknown-sector goal. Favors, warfare, sector purchases, and temporary abandonment protection provide expansion opportunities.
- Reworked abandoned-sector protection:
  - Recently abandoned sectors remain diplomatically contested for four hours. Conquering one before the claim expires can provoke its former owner.
  - The separate NPC reclaim cooldown is reduced from 24 hours to a two-hour baseline: 2.5 hours on Easy, 2 hours on Normal, 1.5 hours on Hard, and 1 hour on Insane.
  - Fairness favor respects the reclaim cooldown.
- NPC factions can place claims on Xenon sectors outside the Xenon Crisis without waiting for Xenon territory to expand. Faction-specific restrictions still apply.
- Reduced the random factory disassembly cooldown from nine to eight hours to match strategy updates.
- Aggressive Task Forces can raid enemy factions, ported from FoF.
- Ships return fire when attacked while engaging defenseless targets. Capital ships prioritize hostile Huge Ships and OWPs before factories and other stations.
- Improved NPC ship selection:
  - Missile boats are eligible across all supported classes instead of only M7s.
  - Regular ships and missile boats can substitute for each other when the preferred type is unavailable.
  - Miner selection and M8 missile-boat requirements remain strict.
- Defensive squadrons target hostile Jump Beacons in faction territory. Border Patrols and Rearguards also check after patrols; dedicated cleanup remains with Task Forces, Police, and defensive Raiders.
- Raider fleets support OCV hunters during the Xenon Crisis. Composition increases from 2 M3s and 8 M4s to 3 M3s and 9 M4s.
- During the Xenon Crisis, factions must destroy every Xenon station in a sector before conquering it.
- Nomad Traders can use the Nexus SF.

Xenon defenses and invasions
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- Initial garrisons spawn per sector rather than per station. Initial and newly conquered Xenon sectors use the same station-defense composition across galaxy types.
- Station defenders use spawn budgets for M7s and M2s rather than rerolling each selection.
- Garrison scaling depends on Xenon territory. Invasion scaling depends on difficulty and no longer on Xenon territory.
- Peaceful and Chaotic starts use a normalized territory baseline for extra-garrison limits and reinforcement frequency.
- After a Xenon Crisis, all galaxy types reset extra-garrison scaling from galaxy size rather than remaining Xenon territory.
- Increased baseline extra-garrison coverage from 30% to 60% of Xenon sectors:
  - Easy: 45%.
  - Normal: 60%.
  - Hard: 75%.
  - Insane: 100%, or 60% if the player joined the OCV.
- Extra-garrison build time varies from one to three hours, with a two-hour baseline.
- Insane uses upgraded OCV ships for defenders and further increases fleet strength.
- Each Xenon sector receives at least one M7-class defender at initialization. Three non-portal sectors receive stronger fleets, and the portal sector receives a dedicated reinforced fleet.
- Reduced ship counts in large Xenon invasions while using more elite ships on higher difficulties, including carrier fighters.
- Xenon capital ships no longer randomly abandon station attacks. Removed the previous Easy, Normal, and general retreat chances.

OCV production and invasions
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- Removed the 75% chance for OCV invasions to ignore player sectors on Easy.
- On Easy and Normal, OCV deep-strike protection applies to empires with four or fewer sectors instead of nine. Adjacent invasions remain possible.
- Difficulty-based invasion range and Xenon Jump Beacon targeting are unchanged.
- The portal prioritizes smaller ships when their numbers are below requirements.
- Increased production outside Chaotic galaxies:
  - M1 build time: 4 -> 1.5 hours.
  - M2: 6 -> 2.5 hours.
  - M7: 2 -> 1 hour, with two built at a time.
  - M6: 1 hour -> 30 minutes.
- In Origin galaxies, the first 24 hours use 75% slower OCV production instead of stopping production completely.
- If the player joins the OCV, OCV production is 33% slower, alongside the existing weaker fleets.
- Xenon and OCV fleets reform as evenly as possible. Reformed OCV invasion fleets with fewer than ten ships return to defend the portal if it still exists.
- Correctly mark invading ships and handle conquest when invaders destroy the sector's last station.
- Replaced the old OCV defender state with Xenon defenders and waiting OCV ships.
- Portal-spawned Xenon Js carry LX fighters instead of #deca.cefa.

Kha'ak raids

- Raiders spawn together and form a fleet instead of patrolling separately.
- Standard raids include Destroyers and heavier escorts.
- The quest uses approximately three times the standard raid composition.
- Raid strength scales by difficulty: 75% on Easy, 100% on Normal, 133% on Hard, and 175% on Insane.
- The rework gives Xenon a stronger defensive role, OCV a stronger offensive role, and Kha'ak more cohesive swarm attacks.

Combat and AI fixes

- Fixed NPC non-missile boats being unable to fire missiles while OOS. This restores NPC and player parity.

Standardized ship variants
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Bonuses and costs below are relative to the corresponding base ship.
- Miner: -10% cost, -50% cargo, and +33% hull.
- Hauler: +33% cargo at the base cost. M8, TM, TS, and TL Haulers instead cost +20% because cargo is central to their role.
- Sentinel: +7.5% cost and +33% hull.
- Vanguard: +5% cost and +50% shield generator.
- Raider: +10% cost, +8% speed, and +20% agility. The Minotaur Raider and Mamba Raider cost approximately +15% because of their other properties.
- Advanced: +35% cost; half of the Raider, Sentinel, and Vanguard bonuses; and an improvement addressing a significant design weakness.
  - Population cost matches regular variants.
  - Removed the additional +35% maintenance penalty.
  - Research penalty: 6x -> 5x.
  - Shield regeneration is normalized when shield slots increase.
  - Improvements generally address TS defensive armament; M6, TP, and TM shielding; or M3 armament and speed where the faction lacks a faster M3.

Advanced variant exceptions
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- Chokaro: no speed or agility bonuses; +3 fighter slots, +50% shields, and +50% cost rather than +35%.
- Lofn: no shield bonus; +17% speed rather than +4%.
- Hydra: no shield bonus; CIG turrets and +10% speed rather than +4%.
- Heavy Nemesis: no shield bonus; +5 marine capacity and +15% speed, supporting boarding and compensating for the lack of a Paranid TP+.
- Heavy Osprey: +7 shield slots; costs 20% more than before 2.2.
- Thor: +50% hull instead of +17%, plus one shield slot; no expanded weapon selection.
- Barracuda: +15% speed instead of +4%; no expanded weapon selection.
- Mamba: +1 shield slot and +25% hull instead of +17%; no expanded weapon selection or inherited Raider turret.
- Kea: +1 shield slot, +25% hull, and PBE compatibility. Its +25% cost premium makes it the cheapest Advanced M3.

Some turret compatibility changes, including CIGs on Terran M6s and M7s, prepare for the planned laser research system.

Laser balancing
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- On Easy, claimed ships no longer regain weapons automatically. Easy still retains most lasers under the normal salvage rules.
- Trading Stations and Pirate Bases no longer sell lasers.
- Reworked laser categories and compatibility in preparation for 2.3's planned laser research.
- Alpha Particle Cannon (APC): reworked as an all-rounder; +20% damage per projectile and DPS.
- Plasma Gun (PG): hull-to-shield damage ratio changed to 1:2.
- Mass Driver (MD): reworked as an all-rounder with equal shield and hull DPS.
  - Firing rate: 22 -> 24 (+9% DPS).
  - Projectile speed: 2.5 -> 2.4 km/s.
  - Range: 4 -> 5.04 km.
- Pulsed Beam Emitter (PBE): reclassified as an M4 weapon; hull-to-shield damage ratio 1:2; DPS 880 -> 700.
- High Energy Plasma Thrower (HEPT): reworked as a generic M3 weapon; DPS reduced by 9%.
- Revised faction-specific M3 weapons relative to the generic baseline:
  - PRG: +5% damage; the additional "+15% faster" comparison requires clarification in the verification appendix.
  - Ion Disruptor: unchanged.
  - PBG: +15% damage; range 3.9 -> 3.825 km; firing rate 20 -> 17 rounds per minute, emphasizing burst damage over sustained fire.
  - FBL: +20% damage and -40% projectile speed relative to the comparison baseline. Compared with the previous release, projectile speed rises from 500 to 600 m/s; range is unchanged. Confirm the comparison baseline in the verification appendix.
  - EBC: +4% damage and +15% range.
  - EMPC damage is unchanged; range 3.9 -> 4.05 km and cargo class M -> L. This retains support for weaker Terran M3s such as the Scimitar.
- MAML damage: +4%.
- CIG cost: +7%, between MAML and IPG.
- Increased firing rates:
  - PSG: 16 -> 27 rounds per minute (+69% DPS).
  - PPC: 20 -> 23 (+15% DPS).
  - IPG: 11 -> 14 (+27% DPS).
  - GRC: 14 -> 15 (+7% DPS).
- Repair Laser relative value: 8 -> 32 (4x).
- PBG and EMPC range changes reinforce the Split's burst-oriented combat role. Equal hull and shield DPS gives the MD a clearer role alongside PAC and IRE, including for NPC M5s and Traders without weapon specialization.

Missiles
~~~~~~~~

- Missiles have been removed from Trading Stations to encourage the player to get the perk or venture to Pirate Bases to purchase missiles.
- M8s spawn with 120 chaff to fulfill their role as missile interceptors.
- Ships no longer spawn with random quantities of missiles.
- Free CCDS is now exclusive to NPCs, encouraging the player to invest either in M8 or buy CCDS.
  - By extension, player ships no longer come with free chaff unless the ship is an M8 because the M8 comes with Chaff.
- Fixed Huge Ships spawning with fewer Chaff than intended. They were spawning with 19-21 Chaff rather than 40 Chaff.
- Fixed player-built ships setting missile resupply threshold greater than the number of missiles that were successfully added.

SSD
~~~

- Instead of checking for a flag set by equipping the SSD, now checks if an SSD is installed.
- All defender fleets, including Xenon defenders, OCV defenders, and faction fleets in sector defense mode are affected.
  - Fleet leaders have a 10% chance each cycle (approximately 1 minute) to pursue a target equipped with SSD.
    - If in-sector, the chance is halved (10% -> 5%).
  - Fleet followers check for SSD only if they're checking for targets near their leader.
- All ships with an SSD can be checked, not only player ships. This is to preserve parity.
- This justifies the high price tag of 20 Green Crystals for 1 SSD and provides an additional function for the SSD.
- The SSD still spoofs Police patrols if the player is attempting an intel mission.

Truelight Seeker repairs
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Requires at least one Repair Laser in the main battery, allows empty slots, and rejects other laser types.
  - Repairs stop if the loadout becomes invalid.
- Out-of-sector repair speed now scales with the number of installed Repair Lasers.
- Added checks for target destruction and sector separation.
- Standardized repair completion and cancellation notifications.

- Fixed Kha'ak ships coming with Chaff. Ships do not come with Chaff if they cannot fire it.

- Fixed Repair Laser stats being manipulated in weapon data. This ensures that the TLS is compatible with NPC usage.

- Fixed Data Scanner being able to scan OWPs.
  - Also cleaned up OWP checking.

- Kha'ak lasers now have their own category in the compendium and in laser manufacturing.
  - The PBC is no longer in the Medium Laser category.
  - Laser classes are now properly sorted from light to heavy (was sorting laser classes alphabetically).
