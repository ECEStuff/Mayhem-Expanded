Going rogue? The mod has been updated to 1.9. 

A NEW GAME is required due to updating the Flail Barrage Missile, Yaki fleet composition, and missile boats.

If you wish to play the new Xenon Defect gamestart, then you must generate a new galaxy.

Updated ZMap 1.8.3b is included with Add-Ons 1.9.

-----------------------------------------
		ADDITIONS
-----------------------------------------
- Xenon Defect start
  - You start with a Xenon M in a Xenon sector but with -1k reputation with everyone except Yaki and Goner.
  - Instead of spawning a free satellite in space, you start with it in your cargo hold.
  - Xenon and OCV are still enemies with you. This is intended.
  - OCV ships cost +15% maintenance (instead of +35%) due to the foreign ship maintenance penalty.
  - This start gives you a bonus to Xenon research. If you're playing on non-insane difficulty and you choose an extra ship, it will be a Phanon ship. However, you will not get its blueprint.
  - Fame ascension is slightly different: instead of getting reputation with your origin faction, you must gain +6 rank (+7 on insane) with any commonwealth faction.
  - Unlike normal starts, your ascension TL will NOT destroy Xenon in the same sector as it is in when the TL spawns.
  - Messages have slightly changed to tailor toward Xenon defect.

- Flail barrage missile reworked. (Thank you so much, XenonArchitect7, for creating the Flail Missile icon.)
  - It is now a Terraformer (XEN and Phanon) artillery missile, exclusive to the Xenon H and Phanon missile boats. 
  - Pros:
    + Fastest turn rate, turning as fast as fox 1 missiles
    + Highest rate of fire out of all missiles
    + Cheapest artillery missile
    + Highest lifespan (Disruptor has the second-longest at 55s)
    + Lightest artillery missile 

  - Cons:
    - Lowest damage per hit and damage per minute (DPM)
    - Slowest artillery missile

  - Stats changed:
    - Damage per hit: 8037 -> 40000
    - Speed: 972 m/s -> 1800 m/s
    - Lifespan: 26s -> 60s
    - Volume: 12 -> 24
    - Blast radius: 5000 -> 10000
    - Cost: 16 silicon -> 37 (approx. 2.25x as much as before)

    - Aquata (3x200 MJ -> 2x200 MJ)

  - Explanation: This makes M8s more fragile, fitting their role as consumables: they're good at frontloaded damage but designed to be weak should they receive incoming fire. As for Phanon ships, this gives their missile boats consistency. The Solaris, while it has a missile turret, is unchanged due to having decent frontloaded damage for its cost.

- Nation milestones changed. They now scale with difficulties. Default values are now as follows:
  - Fleet power: 2000 -> 1500
  - Sectors: 8 -> 3
    - 3 sectors is considered the standard number of sectors for an NPC faction to be on its own, assuming that their sector stats are at least average. If so, then they don't get free crafting.
      - This ensures parity between the player and AI.
    - Hard guarantees nation status upon reaching 2 sectors at most, checked after scaling.
    - Insane guarantees nation status upon reaching 1 sector at most, checked after scaling.
  - Cash: 20M -> 4M
  - Resources: 2M -> 1M. Now considers blue and green crystals in addition to shipbuilding materials.

  - Difficulty scales these milestones as follows:
    - Easy: 1.34x
    - Normal: 1x (baseline)
    - Hard: 0.67x
    - Insane: 0.34x
