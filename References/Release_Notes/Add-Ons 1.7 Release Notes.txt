- Supply Cap (inspired by Hector's suggestion: https://forum.egosoft.com/viewtopic.php?p=5298579#p5298579)
  - When you start the game, you start with 150 supply. This is scaled by difficulty.
  - Each outpost you have gives you supply equal to their maximum population * 10.
  - If you exceed the supply cap, then all your ships' maintenance and crafting costs will increase proportionately. 
    - For example, if you're 20% above your supply cap, then all your existing ships cost 20% more maintenance. New ships will cost 20% more materials than usual.
  - Every ship you own costs supply equal to (ship's relative value / 100). This includes ships you claim, board, build, buy, etc.
  - Supply hard caps at 1 million after which additional outposts you build will no longer give you additional supply.
  - You can view your supply usage in 4 places: Outpost GUI, Ministry of War, Maintenance GUI, and whenever you receive the 12-hour report.
  - Xenon ships consume 25% less supply. OCV ships consume 50% more supply.
    - OCV ships have this penalty to offset their extreme cost-efficiency and swarm potential.
    - Xenon ships have this buff to compensate for their lack of flexibility, population cost (they don't use crew), and high base ship costs.
  - NPC factions, including OCV and the Renegades, now respond to your supply usage. If you exceed your supply cap, they will build more fleets.
    - For Renegades, their ship quality improves.
  - Supply cap scales with difficulty:
    - Easy: 6x
    - Normal: 3x
    - Hard: 2x
    - Insane: 1x

- (1.7.4) Changes
  - Ship base stats changed:
    - OCV O 
      - Increased acceleration (40m/s^2 -> 45m/s^2) and angular acceleration (40 -> 45)
      - Explanation: This ship is now a proper OCV M4: it is now more mobile but fragile compared to the other 2 OCV M4s.
    - OCV OX 
      - Decreased speed by 7.5% (398m/s -> 368.1m/s) 
      - Decreased acceleration (45m/s^2 -> 40m/s^2) and angular acceleration (45 -> 40)
      - Explanation: This ship was practically a direct upgrade to the O since it previously had both the improved speed and hull, save for a slightly worse turn rate. It's been changed to a sidegrade of the O: more durable in exchange for mobility.
    - OCV #deca_deaf
      - Increased shield generator (80 -> 150)
      - Increased shield slots (4 -> 6)
      - Explanation: This ship was more expensive than the O/OX while having less effective durability per cost and less burst DPS. As such, it's been updated to be more like an M4+ to justify its price tag. Compared to the O and OX, it retains its role as a more efficient brawler against enemy fighters.
    - Argon Phantom
      - Increased relative value by 50% (7423 -> 11134)
      - Explanation: Players could build a lot of these ships as poor man's siege units, especially so on insane mode where a capital shipyard is required to build capital ships. The cost increase is to encourage the player to build capital ships as proper siege units.
    - Truelight Seeker
      - Decreased number of speed extensions needed for max speed (1 -> 0).
      - Explanation: This ship had an inconsistency with maximum number of speed extensions (required 1 for max speed).
    - Ranger
      - Increased cargo bay size (75 -> 220)
      - Increased speed (510m/s -> 561m/s)
      - Upgraded cargo type (M -> L).
      - Explanation: This ship was outclassed even by TEL M5s that were not only cheaper but also faster and more spacious. The ranger been buffed to a pure scout/courier to justify its price tag.
    - Springblossom (including raider variant)
      - Doubled cargo bay size (1395 -> 2790)
      - Explanation: This M6 is the only M6 to lack compatible missiles. While it has decent DPS, it lacks the durability or speed suitable for combat compared to other M6s. To fit with the Private Corp theme of utility, this ship is now an M6 with increased cargo capacity, essentially making it an armored freighter with its role similar to its Mayhem 2 incarnation.

- Renegades Plot
  - Renegade ship quantity (assassins; see below) and quality (all renegade ships) scale based on difficulty and supply usage. 
    - Scaled renegades may spawn with an extra pandora tuning (on top of the existing pandora tuning), thus having a maximum of 2 pandora tunings upon spawn.
  - The renegade station now spawns additional ships, scaled based on the player's fleet power (FP). This scales based on difficulty.
    - Easy: 1 extra ship per 200 FP
    - Normal: 1 extra ship per 150 FP
    - Hard: 1 extra ship per 120 FP
    - Insane: 1 extra ship per 90 FP
  - Difficulty modifiers changed: 
    - Easy spawns 20% less (previously no reduction)
    - Hard now spawns 20% more ships (previously no extras)
    - Insane now spawns 50% more (previously 33% more)
  - Can now spawn 2-15 ships per cycle (previously only 2). 
    - The more ships they can support, the more ships they can spawn per cycle.
    - Cannot spawn more than 2 M7 per cycle (hard and insane difficulties only).
  - Invasion timer is now based on difficulty. Easy: 4 hours; normal: 3 hours; hard: 2 hours; insane: 1.5 hours.
  - Renegade defenders now decide whether to invade or defend by comparing your fleet power inside their sector against theirs.
    - Previously, they compared your fleet power against a set value.
    - Now, if the player has at least X% fleet power compared to defenders, then defenders will stick to defense. % scales by difficulty.
      - Higher difficulties increase this threshold.
        - Easy: 40%
        - Normal: 50%
        - Hard: 60%
        - Insane: 75%

	. Renegades.Task.Station.xml

  - Renegade roamers overhauled. 
    - Renegades roamers factor not only game difficulty but also sector size.
    - There is no more absolute minimum or maximum number of roamers. As soon as there are enough roaming renegades or if the player's FP is high enough, more renegades will still spawn, and these will be sent to raid the player's sectors.
    - Multiple roamers can now spawn every cycle, again scaled depending on galaxy size and game difficulty.
    - Renegade raiders (ships sent to attack the player) now use the same ship classes that renegade defenders (renegades spawned by the renegade station) use.
    - Like defenders, roamers now scale with FP. If the player's FP is too high, these will send tougher ships as raiders.
      - For example, the game may spawn an M7 roamer even on normal game difficulty because the scaled difficulty is hard.
    - The game will notify the player when the first renegade is sent to attack the player's sectors.
