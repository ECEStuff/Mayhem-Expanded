-----------------------------------------
		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------
- Pop growth mechanics
  - Increased first population growth (20 -> 40 pops).
  - Increased first growth cutoff (30 -> 40 pops).
  - Rationale:
    - It will now take 1 growth cycle (previously 2 cycles) before standard growth rate is used.
    - However, after this initial growth cycle, future growth cycles will use the standard growth rate rather than the boosted rate.
    - 2 20-pop growth cycles allowed pop manipulation exploit (intentionally reducing population to take advantage of significant growth rate). 1 40-pop growth cycle ensures that the Outpost has its bootstrap growth used to start the Outpost rather than be used for infinite pops (OP when combined with pop transfer and no food consumption, allowing infinite pops on demand).
