The mod has been updated to 1.9.3. 

Previous savegames compatibility: PARTIAL. 
- Reasons:
  - Stock exchange now functions as a proper trade station (buy/sell wares).
  - Trade stations now have the correct missiles assigned to them.
- A new game is not required but recommended due to this patch being applicable to existing games.

Previous galaxies compatibility: YES.

-----------------------------------------
		ADDITIONS
-----------------------------------------
- OCV spawning
  - Added #deca_cefa and #deca_deaf to OCV spawns, allowing the OCV to use tougher fighters.
  - Added difficulty scaling to OCV initial spawns in chaotic galaxy gamestarts. Normal is baseline (1.0x).
    - Easy: -20% spawns
    - Hard: +20% spawns
    - Insane: +50% spawns

- Fighter bailing rework
  - Bail chance is now determined upon creation rather than while fleeing. 
  - Unlike capital ships, fighters do not bail on death. The only time they will bail is if they're fleeing.
  - Teladi fighters are now 33% more likely to bail.
    - This gives parity with Teladi capships bailing more often; it's Teladi philosophy to be cowards.

- Unfortunate events and Insurance Policy rework
  - On hard and insane, rather than 85% reduced chance of unfortunate events, now reduces the severity by 85% (70% on insane).
  - Updated Deadly Disease and Major Setback event descriptions.
  - Deadly disease now blocks population transfer at afflicted Outposts.
  - Added color coding to Outpost statuses (orange for mitigated events, green for insured Outposts) in the Outpost GUI.
  - With insurance policy on hard and insane:
    - Terraformer virus causes all non-OCV ships built during the event to get an indicator. Timer variance is also reduced.
    - Kernel bug reduces the maintenance penalty from +100% to +15% (+30% on insane).
    - Deadly disease kills 2-3 (4-6 on insane) people per consumption cycle.
    - Storage fire destroys 0.19% (0.38% on insane) of wares per minute.
    - Major setback restores 85% (70% on insane) of lost progress per tick. In other words, progress per tick is ~92% (85% on insane) as much compared to normal.
