- Population growth rate mechanics
  - Terraforming effects now apply to the base growth rate before other modifiers (pop growth scaling and pop growth restriction).
  - Pop growth scaling reworked: every 2 pops above 100 gives the Outpost +1% population growth rate. This affects the bonus given by terraforming.
  - Tax perk no longer reduces growth rate to 1 when above 50% population limit. Now reduces growth rate by 80%. This gives it some synergy with population stability.

	. Mayhem.GetNaturalGrowth.xml

- War declaration mechanics
  - Ultimatums
    - Rank 10 now guarantees an ultimatum, provided that you have at least 2 sectors.
      - If you are rank 10 when you fail to comply with an ultimatum, your reputation will drop to rank 8 (rank 7 on insane). Now displays a special message when this occurs.
    - Ultimatum chance calculation changed. These definitions are used in the comments inside the script.
      - Friendly is defined as having between rank 1 to rank 5 (6 on insane).
      - A close ally is defined as having rank 6 (7 on insane).
      - A closer ally is defined as having rank 7-8 (8 on insane).
      - A strong ally is defined as having rank 9.
      - Reduced loyalty point effectiveness: each point now gives +1% ultimatum chance (was previously 2%).
      - If you are not rank 10 with a faction, then the chance is calculated as follows:
        - Friendly: +2% ultimatum chance per rank
        - Close ally: +5% ultimatum chance per rank
        - Closer and strong ally: +10% ultimatum chance per rank
        - Being a strong ally makes evil factions (these have negative ethos as defined in the 9973 t-file) more likely to give you an ultimatum. Specifically, it reduces the ethos penalty to 1/3 as much. (Positive ethos doesn't reduce ultimatum chance; negative ethos does.)

    - Rank 9+ further reduces rank chance: now reduces war chance by 80% (previously reduced by only 60%).

- Ship stats changed
  - Albion Pride: Marine capacity: 0 -> 5
    - Explanation: Fits its lore as a shuttle, not as a warship.
  - OWPs:
    - Large (unused): 6x2 GJ -> 7x2GJ
    - Small (unused) and Medium: Now compatible with IBL, CIG, GRC, and MAML. Removed all other weapons from turrets.
    - Now come with the same set of wares as most ships (previously had an unused set of wares).
    - Explanation: Gives OWPs more DPS and range but still nowhere near dedicated capital-class weapons. This is to force engagements against OWPs. Also, these are neutral ships, so they use neutral (commonwealth) weapons rather than race-specific ones.
