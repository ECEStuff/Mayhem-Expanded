Expanded has been updated to 2.2.1.

Previous savegames compatibility: YES.  
Previous galaxies compatibility: YES.

Highlights
- LIECNSE.md has been updated to further clarify its scope and permissions.
- Azimuth has been updated to 2.0.2.2a. See included Azimuth release notes.
- Additional QOL + script cleanup.

		CHANGES
-----------------------------------------

- Licensing (see LICENSE.md)
  - Clarified the scope of the license and its relationship to predecessor projects.
  - Clarified permissions for independent forks, add-ons, compatibility patches, and individual script reuse.
  - Clarified attribution, project identity, and protection against artificial access restrictions.
  - Explicitly permitted independent gameplay redesigns.
  - Clarified editable mod-file distribution requirements and noncommercial use.

- Arithmetic hardening and percentages
  - Moved percentage formatting to SSOT.
  - Introduced a centralized integer scaling algorithm using quotient–remainder decomposition and overflow-safe binary multiplication.
  - Hardened economic, production, taxation, supply, recycling, terraforming, and hacking calculations against intermediate integer overflows.
  - Hardened NPC fleet requirements, overextension scaling, OCV invasion quotas, and OCV production cycles.
  - Improved arithmetic safety in sector claim durations and Xenon invasion cycles.
  - Added safeguards against invalid divisors in supply and galaxy-size scaling calculations.
  - Optimized overextension calculations by bounding the target scaling factor before calculating potentially excessive supply percentages.
  - Preserved existing gameplay formulas, rounding behavior, and difficulty modifiers.

  - Successful boarding now incurs an additional reputation penalty on subsequent captures.

  - Docked fighters suffer 80% hull damage during the takeover, while deployed fighters retain their existing hull.
    - Captured support fighters are reconstructed to remove their previous squadron associations, with eligible Support commands restored afterward.
